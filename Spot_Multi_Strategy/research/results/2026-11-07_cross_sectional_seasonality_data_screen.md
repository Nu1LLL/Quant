# Data-only screen for a future cross-sectional weekday-seasonality hypothesis

The economic idea comes from [Long et al. (2020)](https://repository.up.ac.za/server/api/core/bitstreams/43ef1895-818c-44bb-9c3c-848aa9f670f5/content), which used the previous 20 same-weekday daily returns in a cross-sectional ranking. **No price return, signal ranking, trade, or PnL from our proposed 2021–2026 holdout has yet been read.** No strategy is registered by this document.

The [2020-12-30 capitalization snapshot](https://coinmarketcap.com/historical/20201230/) and the earlier timestamp-only archive screen defined ten candidate USD-M perps. A subsequent complete numeric source audit of **all 610 pinned five-minute archives from 2020-12 through 2025-12** rejected `DOTUSDT` and `XTZUSDT`: each has one archive row whose taker-buy base volume exceeds total base volume (DOT at 2023-11-14 11:15 UTC; XTZ at 2023-09-21 07:05 UTC). This is a source-quality exclusion made before opening any weekday-seasonality strategy results, not an asset performance choice. The other eight (`ADAUSDT`, `BCHUSDT`, `BNBUSDT`, `BTCUSDT`, `ETHUSDT`, `LINKUSDT`, `THETAUSDT`, `XMRUSDT`) passed all 61 months and 534,816 bars per coin.

The separate 2026 data-only extension verified the official checksum, complete five-minute timestamp grid, and numeric bar invariants for all eight coins for **January through September 2026** (72 additional ZIPs). All eight October five-minute archives have the same **missing UTC day, 2026-10-28**: after the 2026-10-27 23:55 bar, the next bar is 2026-10-29 00:00. The October archives therefore fail despite matching the official checksums. No interpolation, day deletion, or October strategy evaluation is allowed. Any future registration may use at most the continuous interval ending **2026-10-01 00:00 UTC**, provided it is fixed before opening strategy results. Funding timestamp archives for January–October 2026 pass the separate rate-blind audit, but no funding-rate magnitudes have been read.

The eligible eight-coin historical price window is continuous from 2020-12-01 through 2026-09-30. It is a source-quality finding only. Binance ZIP checksum validity is not proof of historical executable bid/ask, depth, or guaranteed fills.
