# WhaleWatcher official macro archive: value-blind first-seen bridge

On 2026-11-09 (Shanghai time), Quant added an isolated read-only adapter for WhaleWatcher's `event_state/official_macro_history.json`. The local source was structurally valid and contained **370** officially timestamped historical macro events: **119 CPI, 119 NFP, 132 PCE**. Every imported row has an official BLS or BEA URL, an archive embargo-time basis and a source verification timestamp after the release. No release values, article text, AI output, post-event prices, signal direction, fills or returns were read or copied by the adapter.

The first local capture at **1794156695409 UTC milliseconds** was `fresh` with 370 inserted event identities. The append-only SQLite journal is **132 KiB** after that capture and capped at **16 MiB**. A read at the millisecond before first capture returned **zero** events; a read at the capture time returned 370. All 370 records are explicitly `retrospective_archive=true`, `historical_oos_eligible=false` and `execution_eligible=false`. Their old official publication timestamps are never promoted to Quant first-seen times. Later source revisions or removals are appended as new versions/tombstones rather than rewriting earlier as-of states. An unavailable/malformed source poll does not erase the last captured history.

The module was copied byte-for-byte to the local MiniMedallion package (SHA-256 `938598ce571f65fba21a7b1783c5544c949376a38a660ed37b7fe512beab1f04`). LaunchAgent `com.nu1lll.minimedallion.whalewatcher-official-history` is registered for one poll per day; its first run exited **0**. The upstream WhaleWatcher application and the user's protected mature strategies were not modified. Three new synthetic adapter tests and the existing WhaleWatcher macro/readiness tests passed (13 in the combined focused run).

This is source lineage infrastructure, **not** an economic hypothesis, backtest or five-year independent OOS. A future event strategy still needs its own preregistration, causal feature availability, costs, $10,000 execution checks, annual folds, regimes and Monte Carlo. The separate prospective macro schedule journal remains the source for what Quant knew before an event; this retrospective archive cannot replace it.
