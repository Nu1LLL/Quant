# WhaleWatcher source continuity and point-in-time features

**Status:** research infrastructure only; no new strategy, result, Alpha, order or OOS pass.

The prior append-only journal stored only metadata **changes**. An unchanged feed could leave no new row for hours, so historical source availability could not be audited. The isolated bridge now also appends one small `source_polls` row for every successful collector invocation, including unchanged and unavailable source states. The original event/claim change records remain append-only. `poll_coverage` reports missing periods, source failures and whether a full five-year collection window has actually been observed. The 64 MiB journal cap remains in force.

`whalewatcher_features.py` converts the as-of journal into a fixed 21-field count vector by event category, source and independently verified BTC/ETH claim side. It exposes no article text, URL, ID, market return, forecast or order. The vector is absent when no recent source poll exists; missing data is not a zero-news reading. An event counts as fresh news only if both its original publication and Quant's capture fall within the feature window and their delay is at most two hours. This prevents the initial import of older articles from appearing as a sudden live news burst. Every vector is explicitly research-only and ineligible for historical OOS or execution.
