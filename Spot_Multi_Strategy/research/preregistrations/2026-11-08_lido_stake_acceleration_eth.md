Acceleration of that stock, after accounting for the growing base through percentage changes, is hypothesized to precede stronger ETH spot demand. The signal uses Lido's **Ethereum WETH token units**, rather than USD TVL, and is a liquid-staking participation channel. It is distinct from the prior Ethereum **DeFi lending/collateral TVL** experiment, which explicitly excluded liquid staking; it is also distinct from price-only momentum, stablecoin issuance, futures basis/funding, option IV, exchange inventory and blockchain transaction-activity tests.
