## Economic hypothesis and evidence grade

When regulated CME Bitcoin futures asset managers increase their **net long exposure relative to total open interest**, sustained institutional demand may precede a stronger following Bitcoin spot week. This is a testable transfer hypothesis, not a claim that CFTC classified traders are informed, that their positions are unhedged, or that futures positioning equals aggregate spot flow. It is economically distinct from earlier on-chain staking, exchange inventories, miner margins, network use, DeFi revenue, Binance perpetual funding and price-only studies. CFTC's [TFF description](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/index.htm), [historical archives](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/HistoricalCompressed/index.htm) and [CFTC Bitcoin-trader research](https://www.cftc.gov/sites/default/files/2021-11/WhoTradesBTC_V2_ada.pdf) motivate the source; none establish this rule's profitability.

The downloaded annual archives are **current-vintage historical records**, without preserved first-release snapshots or exact report publication timestamps. The deliberately long lag below is a conservative publication proxy, not proof of point-in-time availability. ## Frozen rule, publication lag and execution

- At each **Monday 00:00 UTC**, choose the latest CFTC report with `report_date <= Monday date - 13 calendar days`; for that report `t`, define `r_t = (Asset_Mgr_Long_t - Asset_Mgr_Short_t) / Open_Interest_t`. Let `r_(t-4)` be the ratio in the **fourth prior report row**, even where a report date is a holiday-shifted Wednesday. Target **100% BTC spot long** iff `r_t > 0` **and** `r_t > r_(t-4)`; otherwise target **100% USDT cash**. Strict equality means cash. No BTC price, carry, funding, macro state, prior PnL, other CFTC trader class or human override enters the signal. The 13-day lag means a report normally released on the Friday after its as-of date gets at least the following weekend plus one week before use; it remains a proxy, not verified vintage timing.
- Execute a changed target **Monday 00:00 UTC** at that day's Binance spot open, with unchanged targets incurring no fill. The one-day-delay stress instead executes on **Tuesday 00:00 UTC** using the same preceding Monday decisions. On 2026-01-01 00:00 UTC, force-sale any BTC at the daily open and assign the terminal proceeds to the 2025-12-31 factor. No fresh terminal entry. Cash earns zero. Long BTC or cash only, gross **≤1x**, no short, futures trade, funding, borrowing, Kelly sizing or live orders.
- Each buy executes at `open × 1.0005`; each sale at `open × 0.9995` (**5 bp adverse slippage per fill**) and pays **10 bp taker fee on executed notional per fill**. Reserve fee, round BTC quantity down to **0.00001 BTC**, retain cash dust. Every fill must be ≥10 USDT. Buy notional must be ≤**1% of the prior 30 completed UTC days' median Binance quote volume**; exceeding it is an execution failure, not a skipped fill. This is a rough capacity estimate, not observed order-book slippage. Daily close marking uses conservative intraday **high-before-low** drawdown accounting, including entry/exit days; require positive equity and no negative cash or >1x gross exposure. Reconcile all 1,826 net daily factors to final equity.
- Fixed comparators: BTC buy-and-hold with identical lot/cost and terminal exit; the same signals at zero costs; **double slippage** (10 bp per fill) and the Tuesday-lag stress, each required to finish above 10,000 USDT. They cannot be selected as an alternate strategy after results.
