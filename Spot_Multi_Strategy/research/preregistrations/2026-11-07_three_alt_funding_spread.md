## Economic hypothesis and limitations

[He, Manela, Ross and von Wachter (2022), *Fundamentals of Perpetual Futures*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06888) explain the payment from long to short perpetual holders when funding is positive and the economic pressure that narrows the perpetual–spot gap. Here the **new testable transfer hypothesis** is narrower and not established by that paper: a 30-day cross-sectional difference in *already settled* funding persists into the next calendar month enough for a dollar-neutral short of the higher-rate altcoin and long of the lower-rate altcoin to cover realistic trading costs and price-basis risk. It differs economically from prior jump/flow reversal and BTC/ETH price-distance experiments; those previous experiments only treated funding as a cost. A published [2026 negative replication of short-horizon OHLCV/funding screens](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6701738) lowers confidence rather than supplying a filter or parameter choice. No signal weight or asset is chosen after seeing results.

The fixed lexically ordered universe is Binance USD-M perpetual **ADAUSDT, BNBUSDT, DOGEUSDT**. No spot, BTC, ETH, news, WhaleWatcher, AI interpretation, or cross-venue data enter this formal return calculation. Other source streams remain independent shadow research.

## Frozen monthly decision and execution

1. Formal account interval is **[2021-01-01 01:00, 2026-01-01 01:00) UTC**, exactly 1,826 complete 01:00-to-01:00 UTC days and five complete annual folds. January 2021 remains flat while funding history accumulates. At **17:00 UTC on the final UTC day of every month from January 2021 through November 2025 inclusive**, form the next-month decision. There are exactly **59** decision opportunities for entries 2021-02-01 01:00 through 2025-12-01 01:00, one per calendar month. No other entry/rebalance times.
2. For each symbol at each decision, take the arithmetic mean of its **90 immediately preceding completed eight-hour funding settlements**, ending with that day's 16:00 event. Missing history or invalid rate fails the data Gate. Sort by `(mean_rate, symbol)` ascending. Candidate long is the lowest mean, short is the highest; exact equal top/bottom mean gives no trade. The symbol tie breaker is fixed lexical order; never rank by future return or actual next-month funding.
3. Let `N=3 × number_of_UTC_days_in_next_calendar_month`, known at decision. A trade is permitted only if projected **1x** funding spread benefit `0.5 × N × (highest_mean − lowest_mean)` is **strictly greater than 0.006**, the fixed approximate 1x roundtrip fee plus adverse execution cost. Otherwise stay in USDT cash for the entire next month. This is a cost screen based only on prior settled rates and known calendar length, not a learned forecast. No sign reversal, secondary threshold, discretionary override or parameter optimization.
4. At next month **01:00 UTC hourly reference open**, enter the long-low/short-high pair at adverse prices. Close at the **following calendar month 01:00 UTC hourly open**, exactly once. A changed signal at the same boundary first exits the old pair, accounts that exit to the ending day/month, then opens the next; no netting benefit is assumed. The 01:00 fill is after the 00:00 funding settlement and after the preceding 00:00–01:00 bar closes. There is no intramonth exit, stop, take-profit, cash sweep or leverage change.

## Fixed capital, financing and pressure marks

- Start with **10,000 USDT**. Formal gross entry notional is **2x** current pre-entry marked equity, split 50/50 between simultaneous long and short. The signal-quality comparator uses **1x** split 50/50. These are below the user's 5x hard cap; never test or raise leverage to rescue a low Sharpe. Quantity is fixed at entry, no rebalancing inside the month. Flat periods hold cash with zero assumed yield. No unmodeled interest is credited.
- Each leg pays **5 bp taker fee each side**, plus an additive adverse price adjustment of **25 bp each side**: buy `p_open × (1+0.0025)`, sell `p_open × (1−0.0025)`. A fixed stress uses **50 bp** adverse price adjustment with the same fee. The 1x flat-price roundtrip drag is about **60 bp** of account equity; 2x about **120 bp**. These are assumed penalties, not measured book depth or proven fills. Report per-scenario fees and funding separately.
- At each actual funding timestamp inside `[entry,exit)`, long pays positive signed rate and short receives it; funding notional is fixed signed quantity times the most recent **completed** hourly trade close. At `t+0…999ms` the reference is hour `[t−1h,t)` with archived close time `t−1ms`, never the new hour. One stress keeps all adverse funding charges but sets favorable funding cash flows to zero. Missing proxy or nonfinite rate fails the data Gate. Binance's true funding mark price and liquidation engine are unavailable and must not be claimed as verified.
- Mark positions on each completed hourly close. For conservative pressure drawdown take each long low and each short high in the **same hour simultaneously**; include the entry hour and exclude the exit hour. At each held hour update the regular-equity peak first with that hour's regular close, then evaluate the adverse mark against the updated peak. At every UTC 01:00 daily boundary, mark at that hour's opening reference; scheduled exits are booked **before** closing the prior day and scheduled new entries **after** starting the next day. Thus funding at UTC 00:00 is attributed to the preceding 01:00-anchored day. A scenario whose regular or adverse equity is nonpositive at any hour/boundary is a sealed failure, never a partial performance claim.

## Frozen Gate, market states and stopping

- Compute 60 consecutive monthly returns and 1,826 consecutive 01:00-anchored daily returns, including January 2021 flat and any skipped months. CAGR is `(E_end/10000)^(365.25/1826)-1`; Sharpe is `sqrt(365) × sample_mean(daily_return)/sample_sd(daily_return)` at zero risk-free rate. Daily dollar-PnL Profit Factor is sum of positive daily dollar PnL divided by absolute sum of negative daily dollar PnL. Undefined/zero-trade PF or Sharpe is insufficient evidence. Report final equity, 1x/2x CAGR, Sharpe, max hourly-pressure drawdown, PF, gross and net PnL, all 60 months, positive/negative/flat count, worst month and longest losing-month streak, trades, fees and funding.
- **1x prerequisite:** CAGR >0, Sharpe >1.5, max drawdown ≤20%, PF >1.3. **2x formal numerical target:** CAGR ≥40% (also report whether ≤60%), Sharpe >1.5, max drawdown ≤20%, PF >1.3. No leverage increase after a failed 1x prerequisite.
- Annual walk-forward reports 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025 as five contiguous **01:00-anchored** annual folds. Each requires at least one entered pair, positive net marked fold return and positive daily Sharpe using the same frozen rule. There is no retraining or asset selection across folds.
- At the **17:00 decision time** classify the market using the equal-weight mean of the three individual **30-day log close returns** ending with the completed 16:00–17:00 hour (`≥0` bull, else bear). Volatility is sample SD of the **336 immediately preceding hourly equal-weight mean log returns** ending at that hour, high if at least the median of the **30 prior daily 17:00 snapshots**, each itself using its trailing 336 hourly returns; otherwise low. December 2020 price history supports the first decision. Missing close/snapshot yields `unknown`, which fails this Gate. Allocate each completed pair's full cost-net PnL to its entry bull/bear × high/low bucket; all four buckets require ≥1 trade and positive summed net PnL.
- Monte Carlo uses **5,000** paths from one `numpy.random.Generator(numpy.random.PCG64(20261110))`. Each draws **87** uniform circular starts from the 1,826 daily records, takes 21 consecutive days per start, concatenates and truncates to 1,826. Store daily end equity factor `a`, maximum regular equity factor `b` including day start, minimum adverse equity factor `c` including day start, and worst within-day adverse-from-prior-regular-peak drawdown `d`. Exact resampled drawdown is the worse of `d` and `1−start_equity×c/prior_global_regular_peak` per day, updating the global regular peak with `b`. Verify compressed replay reproduces original full hourly-pressure drawdown before accepting. At least **80%** of paths must jointly meet the four formal numerical thresholds. Report p05/p50/p95 and undefined counts. Both doubled-nonfee-cost and no-beneficial-funding-credit 2x stresses must end above 10,000 USDT.
