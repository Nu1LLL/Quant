## Mechanism and evidence boundary

[Ethereum's monetary mechanism](https://ethereum.org/roadmap/merge/issuance/) combines new ETH issuance with EIP-1559 base-fee burning. A declining total ledger supply over four weeks is a directly observable **net monetary contraction**, hypothesized to create less dilution and stronger subsequent ETH spot demand. This differs from the earlier three-coin **gross proof-of-work issuance** spread: that experiment did not include burn and did not trade ETH. It also differs from Lido staking stock, DeFi TVL/revenue, price, funding and wallet-flow signals. It may correlate with network usage and broad crypto demand, and supply contraction may already be priced. [Coin Metrics defines `SplyCur`](https://docs.coinmetrics.io/network-data/network-data-overview/supply/current-supply) as native units currently visible on the ledger, **not liquid free float**. Its daily change is an aggregate proxy, not a separately audited decomposition of issuance, base-fee burn, slashing and accounting changes. The direct `SplyBurntNtv` metric requires paid access and is **not** silently substituted. None of these sources proves predictive return alpha.
