# Preregistration: eight-coin same-weekday cross-sectional seasonality

**Experiment ID:** `eight_coin_weekday_seasonality_2026_11_07`. This is one new economic hypothesis, fixed before any return, signal ranking, trade, or PnL from this test is read. This document must be committed and pushed in its own commit before the formal reader/simulator runs. A failed run is sealed; do not change its assets, lookback, dates, costs, execution, Gate or stop rules on the same history.

## Economic source, independence and counterevidence

[Long, Zaremba, Demir, Szczygielski and Vasenin (2020)](https://repository.up.ac.za/server/api/core/bitstreams/43ef1895-818c-44bb-9c3c-848aa9f670f5/content) report that the mean return on the **same weekday over the previous 20 weeks** positively ranks next-day returns among 151 cryptocurrencies in 2016–2019. Their long-short quintile portfolio is an asset-pricing result, not a costed Binance futures execution proof. A [2019 BTC intraday/weekday study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612319301710) found no persistent time-specific *BTC return* pattern, which lowers confidence in a durable trade. This experiment tests cross-sectional **recurring weekday demand**, economically separate from prior short-horizon price-jump reversal, cross-coin lead-lag, funding carry and pair-distance hypotheses. The original paper's 2016–2019 sample and 2020 publication precede every formal evaluation day below; its historical effect is not used as our measured return.

## One frozen signal and position schedule

At each eligible day `d 00:00 UTC`, calculate for each coin
`SEAS[i,d] = mean(R[i,d-7], R[i,d-14], ..., R[i,d-140])`.
All 20 returns end at least six days before the decision, so future-bar leakage is impossible. Sort `(SEAS, symbol)` ascending. Short the bottom two and long the top two, with deterministic lexical tie-breaking. Each leg starts at **25% of current account equity**, giving equal 50% long and 50% short notional, **1.0x gross and 0x net**, under the user's hard 5x leverage cap. No sign flip, momentum filter, machine learner, adaptive rank cut, signal threshold, leverage escalation, Kelly sizing, stop-loss or take-profit is allowed in this experiment. Prior findings on other strategies cannot change this schedule. Repeat the ranking daily, closing every old leg and opening every new leg even if the selected symbol is unchanged.

Before entry, the previous **288 completed five-minute bars** for each selected coin must have a median quote-asset volume of at least **50 times that leg's USDT notional** (at most 2% of the trailing median bar). If any leg fails, remain entirely in cash for that day; no partial portfolio or asset substitute. This is computed from past bars only. Report actual entry and exit bar participation separately as an execution-risk diagnostic, without filtering based on future volume.
The capacity decision uses the preceding `base_1x` account equity and is replayed unchanged in all stress scenarios; each scenario sizes its executed legs to its own preceding account equity.

The decision at `d 00:00` is filled at the `d 00:05` bar **open**; all four legs exit at the `(d+1) 00:05` bar **open** (24-hour holding time). This also creates a five-minute decision-to-fill delay. Settlement funding events strictly after entry and at or before exit are applied to each open leg using the published rate and contemporaneous archived open as the funding notional: long pays positive rate, short receives it; negative rates reverse this. Require exact source event schedule; no presumed zero funding and no funding credit before an actual event. The last entry/exit stays entirely inside September 2026.

## Costs, marks, account and stress

- Start with **$10,000** USDT, no deposits, withdrawals, compounding outside account, idle interest or maker rebate. Roundtrip each leg with **5 bps taker fee per fill** plus **5 bps adverse slippage per fill**; with 1x gross this is approximately 20 bps account cost per fully traded day before funding. Apply the fee to actual executed notional at both entry and exit. Long buys above/open sells below; short sells below/open buys above. These conservative fixed price impacts are assumptions, not historical executable quotes.
- Cash, long/short mark-to-market, funding and costs must reconcile to final equity. Mark every held five-minute bar at archived closes and an adverse long-low/short-high combination. Maximum drawdown uses the worse intrabar mark versus the prior regular equity peak; daily factor compression must replay it exactly. Bankruptcy or any nonpositive intrabar account equity is a strategy failure, not a clipped loss.
- Run four frozen scenarios on the **same decisions and capacity gate**: `base_1x` (5-bp slippage), `double_slippage_1x` (10-bp slippage), `extra_bar_latency_1x` (entry at `d 00:10`, fixed exit `d+1 00:05`), and `no_funding_credit_1x` (charge funding debits but suppress favorable credits). The latter three must each end above $10,000. No performance result is used to choose among them.
- Historical exact contract lot size, historical bid/ask depth, order-book queue, and live fill latencies are unavailable in these archives. Ex-post bar participation is diagnostic only; this cannot be called verified executable OOS.

## Prespecified Gate and failure handling

The primary **net** `base_1x` full-window Gate is CAGR **≥40%** (40–60% is the preferred range), daily Sharpe **>1.5**, adverse five-minute max drawdown **≤20%**, and daily *dollar PnL* Profit Factor **>1.3**. All flat and loss days count. At least **365** of the 1,958 eligible entry days must trade. Each of the five full rolling-year folds must have at least one trade, positive net return and positive daily Sharpe; the 133-day terminal holdout must have positive net return. Report all 65 calendar months, with at least **39 positive months** and longest losing-month streak **≤3** as an additional monthly-stability gate. These thresholds are fixed before outcome inspection.

Classify each day with only completed BTC information into four bull/bear × high/low-volatility states: bull if `ln(P[BTC,d]/P[BTC,d-30]) > 0`; high volatility if the sample SD of `R[BTC,d-30]` through `R[BTC,d-1]` exceeds the median of the **90** rolling 30-return sample SD estimates ending on days `d-1` through `d-90` inclusive. Ties are bear/low. `P[BTC,d]` is observed at the decision boundary, five minutes before entry; no held-period return enters the state. All lookbacks must be available at the first formal day; every state must contain at least **90** formal days and have positive aggregate net dollar PnL on its entry-day trades. Report cash days too.

Run a deterministic **5,000-path circular 21-day block bootstrap** over the exact 1,959 daily factors and compressed intraday adverse marks, using NumPy PCG64 seed **20261112**. At least **80%** of resampled paths must jointly satisfy CAGR≥40%, Sharpe>1.5, drawdown≤20% and PF>1.3. Report p05/p50/p95 of every metric and undefined/ruined path counts. No resampling of selected winners, changed block length, later seed or redefinition after inspection.

Data integrity, account ruin, any missing complete fold/regime, any primary/stress/monthly/Monte Carlo Gate failure, or absent cost/funding reconciliation seals the run as failed.
