## Mechanism and evidence grade

Deribit's BTC/ETH option market quotes expected volatility. When option-implied 30-day volatility substantially exceeds trailing realized volatility for **both** coins, demand for crash protection may reveal risk aversion not fully visible in spot returns. The fixed **untested** prediction is lower subsequent spot-basket risk-adjusted return during that state, so the account holds cash. This is an options-market-information hypothesis, economically different from the earlier price-only, funding/basis, stablecoin-supply, chain-activity, macro and miner tests; neither causal independence nor directional predictive power is assumed. A high implied-realized spread is not by itself a tradable option volatility premium, and the strategy does not sell options.

[Deribit public DVOL endpoint](https://docs.deribit.com/api-reference/market-data/public-get_volatility_index_data) returns daily option-volatility-index OHLC; [Deribit volatility-index discussion](https://insights.deribit.com/education/iv-rank-and-iv-percentile/) describes the option information, not this trading rule. [Official Binance archive](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data) provides spot candles. Both are currently backfilled histories. Original daily first-published DVOL vintages, historical option-book liquidity and executable order-book fills are unavailable; therefore **independent_executable_oos=false** regardless of historical metrics. The historical 2025–2026 holdout is a fixed-rule retrospective transfer, not prospective proof.

## Signal, execution and comparators

At every Sunday `s` from 2021-05-02 through 2026-09-27, use only completed UTC Sunday Binance spot closes and Deribit `1D` bars through `s`. For each coin, calculate 28 daily log returns `log(C_t/C_(t−1))` for `t=s−27..s`; sample standard deviation (`ddof=1`) times `sqrt(365) × 100` is `RV28` in annualized percentage points. `IV` is Deribit Sunday bar **close**. **Target 50% BTC + 50% ETH iff both `IV/RV28 <= 1.25`; else 100% cash.** Equality is risk-on. An invalid or zero `RV28` fails source Gate. No return, volume, funding, future DVOL, market-state label or prior PnL enters the rule. The Sunday close is assumed only available after Monday 00:00 UTC; orders execute **Wednesday 00:00 UTC** at Binance spot daily open, giving at least two full days of delay. An unchanged target produces no fill.

Buy at `open ×1.001`, sell at `open ×0.999` (10 bp adverse slippage), plus 5 bp taker fee per filled notional per side. Buy budgets are half of pre-fill cash; round BTC quantity down to `0.00001` and ETH down to `0.0001`, retain residual USDT. Every fill must be at least 10 USDT and ≤1% of its symbol's prior 30 **completed** UTC days' median Binance daily quote volume, else execution Gate fails; do not skip a prescribed fill. Cash yield zero, no funding on spot. Mark each day to Binance close, retain open/high/low and conservative portfolio high-before-low drawdown. If the basket remains held, forcibly sell at 2026-10-01 00:00 opening, assigning sale fees and proceeds to 2026-09-30 account factor. Account equity ≤0, invalid OHLC, missing UTC day, portfolio leg mismatch or gross >1x fails.

Frozen diagnostics: same-rule 50/50 buy-and-hold from 2021-05-01 to terminal sale with identical costs; zero-fee/zero-slip version; 20 bp adverse slippage per fill; execution one **additional** day late (Thursday), all using the same Sunday signals. Both stressed versions must end above initial capital. These are diagnostics, never alternate candidates.

For all four bull/bear × high/low states require ≥90 account days and positive aggregate net-dollar PnL. States are validation labels only.
