## Mechanism, independence and known weakness

Protocol **revenue generated by actual DeFi usage** may contain information about near-term token demand that is not in TVL, Binance prices, taker flow, funding, basis, wallet reserves, BTC miner economics, macro or attention counts. The frozen hypothesis is *relative*, not a bet that all DeFi tokens rise: the token of the protocol with the strongest recent revenue surprise outperforms the weakest in the next week. This economic source is separate from the already rejected ETH aggregate TVL-flow rule. Protocol parent revenue is **not necessarily paid to holders of its governance token**, and the mapping can change. Some revenue comes from user activity rather than tokenholder cash flow. The paper [*Fundamentals in Cryptocurrency Valuation*](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7566845) reports a short-window association of revenue surprises and token returns; it was posted in October 2026 after most of our test window, so its outcomes **cannot establish independent OOS** here. A separate [revenue-multiple study](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6901559) reports no broad ability for protocol revenue multiples to rank one-year returns, lowering confidence in simple fundamental screens. Our exact cross-sectional seven-day spread is not either paper's portfolio and has not been read for outcomes.

DeFiLlama's free API returns **current backfilled historical summaries**, without each day's original first-published vintage; parent definitions and fee accounting may be revised. Binance historical hourly K-lines and funding events also lack BBO/queue and actual user fills. Set evidence grade `preregistered_retrospective_current_protocol_vintage`, `independent_executable_oos=false` regardless of numerical performance. Future truly prospective WhaleWatcher/revenue capture would require separate data rights and years of observations; this experiment cannot manufacture that evidence.

## Signal, entry, sizing, funding, costs and risk

At each **Wednesday 00:00 UTC** from 2021-01-06 through 2025-12-24, let `s` be the preceding Sunday. For each parent, compute `recent = arithmetic mean(dailyRevenue over s−6..s)` and `prior = arithmetic mean(dailyRevenue over s−27..s−7)`; score `log((recent + 1 USD)/(prior + 1 USD))`. Rank descending, with ties by lexical Binance symbol. Target **long top-ranked**, **short bottom-ranked**; the middle asset stays flat. The $1 pseudocount is fixed before any revenue value is read. A historical Sunday label is treated as available no earlier than Monday, and Wednesday decision gives another day of buffer; actual historical publication delay remains unverified. Execute the pair at **Thursday 00:00 UTC** K-line open; 260 scheduled weekly decisions run Thursday 2021-01-07 through 2025-12-25. If the ordered long/short pair is unchanged, carry its existing quantities with no turnover; if changed, close both legs and open the new pair at that same Thursday open. Force-close any open pair at 2026-01-01 00:00 open. No score threshold, price trend, funding filter, market-state filter or future outcomes enter rankings.

Start at **10,000 USDT**. On entry, allocate 50% of current equity notional to each leg, gross 1x at entry, at most one long and one short. Round each quantity **down to 0.1 token**; require each entry/exit leg ≥10 USDT. Use actual settlement events every 8 hours: long funding cash `−rate × quantity × prior completed hourly close`, short funding cash `+rate × quantity × prior completed hourly close`. At 00:00, settle funding on the position held through the previous hour **before** closing/reopening. The first 2026-01-01 funding is credited/debited to the old pair before forced exit and assigned to 2025-12-31 daily return. No funding is estimated from an average rate. Unused collateral earns zero; no external deposit or borrowing.

Every fill pays **5 bp taker fee** of executed notional. Long buy / short cover uses `hour open ×1.001`, and long sell / short open uses `hour open ×0.999` (**10 bp adverse slippage per fill**). Check proposed entry notional on each leg ≤**1% of prior 30 completed days' median quote-volume** for that symbol; an over-capacity entry seals failure rather than skipping/replacing. The quote-volume cap is a coarse proxy, not proof of order-book liquidity. After every hourly mark, if equity ≤0 or gross market notional / equity >5, seal execution failure; no favorable fictional liquidation. Daily conservative worst mark combines long low and short high within each hour, assumes the high-equity mark occurs before the low-equity mark, and includes fee/funding cash at applicable boundaries. Track daily account equity, fees, funding, reference slippage, quantities, entries/exits and realized PnL. Any input bar or event missing fails.

Two frozen stresses use the same Sunday rankings: (a) double adverse slippage to **20 bp per fill**; (b) one extra day of order latency, **Friday 00:00** instead of Thursday, retaining the same 2026-01-01 terminal close. Both must finish above 10,000 USDT. Cost-free comparator keeps the same rankings and funding but sets fee/slippage to zero. No scenario is chosen after returns are read.

## Complete Gate and stopping

Net account requires CAGR **≥40%** (also report whether inside 40–60% target band), daily Sharpe **>1.5** (`sqrt(365)`, zero risk-free), conservative maximum drawdown **≤20%**, daily-dollar Profit Factor **>1.3**, and ≥20 completed new-pair entries. Each annual 2021–2025 fold must have at least one fill, positive net return and positive daily Sharpe. The fixed 2024–2025 holdout must separately pass all four numeric thresholds. At least **36/60** calendar months must be strictly profitable with no run of more than three nonpositive months; report every year/month, worst month and pair turnover.

For each formal UTC day, classify from the preceding **31 completed daily closes** of all three perpetuals. The equal-weight mean of their 30 log-return series determines market trend: positive total = bull, else bear; sample SD of the equal-weight daily log return × `sqrt(365)` ≥80% is high volatility, else low. Require ≥90 days and positive aggregate net-dollar account PnL in all four states. State is evaluation only. Run exactly **5,000** circular **21-day block bootstrap** paths, NumPy PCG64 seed `20261125`, resampling each net daily factor with normalized high/low factors, rebasing at 10,000 USDT, and recomputing all four numeric metrics. At least **80%** must jointly pass; report p05/p50/p95 plus undefined/ruined counts.
