## Economic mechanism and evidence grade

Rising decentralized-exchange transaction activity may indicate greater use of a chain and fee demand for its native asset. The fixed prediction is **relative**: the chain with the strongest recent DEX-volume expansion outperforms USDT cash over the following week. [Cong, Li and Wang's token-adoption model](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w27222/w27222.pdf) motivates network-usage valuation, **not** this exact volume rule, sign or horizon. DEX volume can be speculative churn, wash trading, or a *reaction* to token price; no causal alpha is assumed. This activity-flow source differs from earlier ETH DeFi **TVL stock**, BSC **stablecoin stock/share**, and AAVE/COMP/CRV **protocol parent revenue** tests. It is not statistically guaranteed independent of those series. No prior result for this exact three-chain ranking has been viewed.

The free [DefiLlama DEX-volume API](https://api-docs.defillama.com/) supplies a **current backfilled historical reconstruction** without verifiable first-published daily vintages. Binance spot daily candles lack BBO/queue and real fill proof. Evidence grade `preregistered_retrospective_current_dex_volume_vintage`; `independent_executable_oos=false` even if all historical metrics are excellent. The 2024–2025 temporal holdout is untouched by this rule but not truly prospective executable OOS. A numerical success can at most motivate a separate prospective paper experiment, never paper/live promotion from this result alone.

## Fixed signal, execution and risk

Each **Wednesday 00:00 UTC** from 2021-01-06 through 2025-12-24, let `s` be the preceding completed Sunday. For each chain compute `recent = sum(DEX USD daily volume on s−13..s)` and `prior = sum(on s−27..s−14)`, then `score = log(recent/prior)`. Rank descending; ties by lexical Binance symbol. If the largest `score >= log(1.25)`, target **100% long** that chain's spot token; otherwise target **100% USDT cash**. The Sunday UTC day is complete at Monday 00:00, **48 hours** before Wednesday's decision; execute a changed target at the following **Thursday 00:00 UTC** daily bar open, 72 hours after Sunday's close. This publication delay is a conservative convention, not proof of the historical API's original release time. No price, future volume, market state or post-entry return enters the selection. If the target is unchanged, carry existing quantity without fees. When switching, sell the old token before buying the new one at that Thursday open. First potential trade is 2021-01-07; forced liquidation is 2026-01-01 open. Cash earns zero.

Each buy invests available cash after fees, rounded down to `ETH 0.0001`, `BNB 0.001`, `TRX 1` token; each required leg must be ≥10 USDT. Charge **10 bp taker fee plus 20 bp adverse slippage per fill**: buy at `open×1.002`, sell at `open×0.998`. Require proposed buy notional ≤**1% of the preceding 30 complete days' median Binance spot quote volume** for that token; capacity failure seals execution, never substitutes another chain. Record residual USDT, quantity, fees, reference slippage, target, fills and daily account marks. No unrealistically free rebalancing, BNB-fee discount or cash yield.

For each open position, if any day's **low ≤88% of its actual entry fill**, schedule a stop sale at the **next day open** using `min(next_open, trigger_day_low, 0.88×entry_fill)×(1−slippage)`, plus the taker fee. This intentionally allows loss beyond 12% on gaps. Stay in cash until the next scheduled Thursday decision; if that new decision still selects the same token, a new entry is allowed. A 2025-12-31 trigger uses the 2026-01-01 first open and is charged to the final 2025 daily factor. Conservative intraday drawdown assumes each held day's high precedes its low. Check positive cash/equity, no margin/short, precise PnL reconciliation and all 1,826 dates.

Fixed comparators/stresses: (a) identical signals with zero fees/slippage; (b) **40 bp** adverse slippage per fill, fees unchanged; (c) execute the same Wednesday decision on **Friday** instead of Thursday, with identical Sunday information and terminal exit; (d) equal-dollar ETH/BNB/TRX spot buy-and-hold from first Thursday to terminal open under baseline fees/slippage. Do not choose a favorable scenario after results. Both stress accounts must end above 10,000 USDT. The signal may be called incremental alpha only if both its net CAGR and Sharpe exceed the three-token buy-and-hold comparator.
