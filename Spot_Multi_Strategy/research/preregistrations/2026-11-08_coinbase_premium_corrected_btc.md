## Prior, prediction and evidence grade

[Stalder and Cosenza (2025)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5070964) document BTC price discovery across Binance and regulated spot venues, with Coinbase's relative influence varying by US fixing time. [Alexander et al. (2024)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4867599) document fragmented price formation across Coinbase and Binance. These papers establish a mechanism, **not** that a daily Coinbase premium predicts next-week returns. The explicit unverified transfer prediction here is: persistently higher Coinbase BTC/USD relative to Binance BTC/USDT, **after correcting for the Coinbase USD price of USDT**, reflects excess USD demand that carries into the following week, so a positive corrected premium favors BTC over cash. A raw BTC/USD minus BTC/USDT gap would confound USDT's dollar value and is excluded.

The five-year data and this preregistration are retrospective. Coinbase historical candles have no verified first-publication vintages or historical BBO/fills, and the 2021-10 sample starts after USDT/USD became available. Set `independent_executable_oos=false` regardless of numerical results. Fixed 2024-01-01–2026-09-30 is a **historical temporal holdout**, not true independent executable OOS; 2026 is after the 2025 price-discovery paper but still historical. A numerical pass would require separate prospective live-data paper validation before any small-position simulation. No real order is authorized.

## One causal signal, execution and costs

For each completed UTC day `d`, compute `premium_d = ln(Coinbase_BTCUSD_close_d / (Binance_BTCUSDT_close_d × Coinbase_USDTUSD_close_d))`. The Coinbase candles carry the day-opening timestamp; their closes are treated as available only **after** that UTC day ends. On **Monday 00:00 UTC**, average the seven premiums from the preceding Monday through Sunday. If the arithmetic mean is **strictly positive**, target long BTC; otherwise target cash. Execute a changed target at **Tuesday 00:00 UTC** Binance spot open, a full additional day after the final signal candle. Do nothing if the target is unchanged. First eligible Tuesday is 2021-10-05. No price trend, premium magnitude threshold, stop, profit target, asset ranking, adaptive parameter or look-ahead publication assumption. Close all BTC at the **2026-10-01 open**; include that final fill and fee in the 2026-09-30 daily end equity for exact account-factor reconciliation.

Each buy fills at `reference_open × (1+0.0005)` and each sell at `reference_open × (1-0.0005)`, **5 bp adverse slippage per fill**, plus **10 bp taker fee on each executed notional**. Use the full available cash including fee reserve, round BTC quantity **down** to the fixed `0.00001 BTC` grid, keep residual USDT cash, and charge the fee in USDT. Require each fill's notional ≥10 USDT. Before a new buy, require its proposed executed notional ≤**1% of the median prior 30 completed Binance daily quote volumes**; failing this is an execution Gate failure, not a reason to skip the day. This is a size proxy and does not prove historical order-book depth or lot/filter rules. No interest on cash, maker rebate, BNB discount, taxes or external deposits. Record exact fill references, quantity, notional, fees, cash, cost-free comparator, and a 2× adverse-slippage stress (**10 bp per fill**). A separate one-day-latency stress uses **Wednesday 00:00 open** for the same Monday signal, holding other decisions and terminal close fixed; both stresses must end above 10,000 USDT.

Daily account return includes all flat days. On held days, mark at the Binance daily close; for drawdown, pessimistically update the running peak with the day's **high before its low**. On entry/exit days include the pre-fill equity and post-fill equity; unknown intraday ordering is treated adversely. Cash/equity ≤0 fails. Daily dollar Profit Factor is sum of positive daily dollar PnL divided by the absolute sum of negative daily dollar PnL. Daily Sharpe uses all 1,826 simple returns, sample SD and `sqrt(365)`, zero risk-free; CAGR uses actual factor count and `365.25` days/year.
