## Mechanism and evidence grade

Aggressive buyers of out-of-the-money BTC puts relative to calls may signal urgent downside insurance demand. **Frozen transfer prediction:** next week's BTC spot return is less favorable when normalized put taker pressure exceeds normalized call taker pressure. This tests Deribit *trade aggressor flow*, distinct in measurement, timing, and venue from the sealed Deribit DVOL/realized-volatility **level** gate and CME classified asset-manager weekly option **position** change. The channels may still correlate; economic/statistical independence is not assumed. Taker direction is not proof of end-investor intent because market makers, spreads, hedges, blocks, and combos can contribute. An [option-smile paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167637724000713) studies net buying pressure and IV slopes, but does not validate this exact spot trading rule. A [recent hourly option-implied-risk paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612326013814) reports limited directional predictability; this is an explicit adverse prior, not a success claim.

[Deribit official history guide](https://statics.deribit.com/files/DeribitInstitutionalSetupGuide.pdf) describes free historical public trades since launch. The [current API schema](https://docs.deribit.com/api-reference/market-data/public-get_last_trades_by_currency_and_time) identifies `direction` as **taker** direction, `amount` as underlying coin for options, and `index_price` as contemporaneous index; it documents a 1,000-trade maximum per response. The historical trade tape is a current-vintage backfill, not a captured real-time first publication. Binance daily candles are also current-vintage public archives. **Evidence grade:** `preregistered_retrospective_cross_venue_option_taker_flow`; **independent_executable_oos=false** regardless of historical metrics. ## Single frozen signal and execution

For every eligible Monday hour, separately for put trades and call trades, set sign `+1` for taker `buy`, `−1` for taker `sell`. Let `P=sum(sign×amount)/sum(amount)` for puts and `C` analogously for calls. The option taker-pressure difference is `D=P−C`. **Target BTC=100% iff both buckets have ≥5 trades and `D<=0`; otherwise target USDT cash=100%.** Equality is long. No implied volatility, price momentum, PnL, later trades, or learned parameter enters the decision. The signal hour ends Monday 13:00 UTC. A target change executes at **Tuesday 00:00 UTC** Binance spot daily open, at least 11 hours later; unchanged target generates no order. For the first Friday–Monday days before the first decision, remain cash.

Buy at `open×1.0005`, sell at `open×0.9995` (5 bp adverse slippage per side) plus **10 bp taker fee** of execution notional per fill. Reserve the fee, round BTC down to `0.00001`, retain USDT dust, require each order ≥10 USDT, and reject any order exceeding 1% of the prior **30 completed days' median BTCUSDT spot quote volume**.
