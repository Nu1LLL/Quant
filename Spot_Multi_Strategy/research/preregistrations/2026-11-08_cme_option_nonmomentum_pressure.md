## Mechanism, literature and evidence boundary

The economic input is **CME Bitcoin options risk transfer by CFTC-classified asset managers**, reconstructed as futures-equivalent combined positions minus futures-only positions. It differs from the previously sealed futures-only net-position rule: option exposure can reflect hedging, convexity and inventory pressure rather than direct futures directional holdings. The two datasets still share the same regulated venue and trader classification, so statistical independence is **not assumed**. [Shen, Li and Luo (2026)](https://hull-repository.worktribe.com/output/5680567/option-positions-non-momentum-trading-and-bitcoin-futures-returns) find a negative association between non-momentum asset-manager option-position changes and second-week-ahead CME futures returns. Our simple 26-report rolling regression and Binance spot/cash account are **a new transfer test, not a replication or causal inference**. The authors examined January 2020–March 2025, overlapping most of our sample, so the paper and our historical holdout cannot supply independent OOS.

Frozen evidence grade: `preregistered_current_vintage_retrospective_option_pressure`; `independent_executable_oos=false`. Annual CFTC ZIPs are current-vintage archives, not immutable first-release snapshots. [CFTC reports](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/index.htm) ordinarily appear Friday for Tuesday positions, but exceptional delays occurred in [2023](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/HistoricalSpecialAnnouncements/index.htm) and [2025](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9147-25). The new decision rule uses `cftc_release_calendar.py` with its pinned delayed-release dates and a full UTC-day publication buffer. For other weeks, an eight-calendar-day delay is a publication proxy, not proof of every true vintage. An earlier futures-only report that omitted these exceptions is separately [invalidated](../results/2026-11-08_cme_cot_release_timing_invalidation.md); its numbers cannot inform this rule.

## Exactly one rolling statistical signal

For report row index `i`, let `C_i` be the Binance UTC daily **close on its report date**; it was available by the later CFTC publication. Define `x_i=ln(C_i/C_(i−1))`. Let `F_i^L,F_i^S` be asset-manager futures-only long/short; `B_i^L,B_i^S` be futures-and-options combined long/short; and `O_i^B` be combined open interest. Define option net ratio `q_i=((B_i^L−B_i^S)−(F_i^L−F_i^S))/O_i^B`, requiring finite `q_i` in `[-1,1]`. Define `d_i=q_i−q_(i−1)`.

At each **Wednesday 00:00 UTC** in the formal interval, select the newest CFTC report `t` with `conservative_eligible_at(report_date; minimum_lag_days=8) <= decision UTC`, including the pinned 2023/2025 exceptional publication dates; an unavailable report is never substituted with a future one. Fit **OLS with intercept** on exactly the **26 prior report rows** `i=t−26,...,t−1` to regress `d_i` on `x_i` (no current or later report in training). If `Var(x_train)<1e−12`, set slope 0 and intercept `mean(d_train)`; otherwise use ordinary least squares. Current residual is `e_t=d_t−(intercept+slope×x_t)`. Target **100% BTC spot long iff `e_t<0`**, otherwise **100% USDT cash**; equality means cash. This inverse sign is a frozen transfer inference from the paper's negative coefficient, not a claimed replication. Hold an unchanged target without trading. Every selected report must have 26 full prior training observations and a prior row for each `d_i`; missing/invalid values fail data/model Gate. Model parameters are refit causally each Wednesday but no hyperparameters are learned or tuned from PnL.

Execute a changed target at that **Wednesday 00:00 UTC Binance open**. The one-day-delay stress executes Thursday open using the same Wednesday decision. Force-liquidate remaining BTC at 2026-01-01 00:00 UTC open and assign proceeds to the 2025-12-31 factor; no fresh terminal entry. Buy at `open×1.0005`, sell at `open×0.9995` (**5 bp adverse slippage each fill**) plus **10 bp taker fee per fill notional**. Reserve fees, round BTC quantity down to **0.00001 BTC**, retain USDT dust, require each fill ≥10 USDT. Proposed buy ≤**1% of previous 30 completed UTC days' median Binance quote volume**, or execution fails. Cash yield zero; no hidden maker rebate. Daily close equity, costs and terminal cash must reconcile; positive equity, nonnegative cash and ≤1x gross. Conservative intraday drawdown uses high-before-low even on fill days.
