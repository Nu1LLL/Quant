## Economic source and evidence

The new input is capital locked in Ethereum DeFi applications, not Binance price, futures funding, spot taker flow, Wikipedia attention, Bitcoin chain use, stablecoin issuance, or a US macro series. DeFiLlama's [free historical chain TVL endpoint](https://github.com/DefiLlama/api-docs/blob/main/llms-free.txt) excludes liquid staking and double counted TVL. The hypothesis is that growth in DeFi collateral **relative to ETH's USD price** proxies net ecosystem capital commitment and predicts a positive subsequent ETH return. Dividing USD TVL by ETH price is only a rough price adjustment: TVL contains other tokens and stablecoins, protocol coverage changes, and this ratio is not a direct cash-flow measurement. [DeFi loanable-funds research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.13922) motivates a separate capital-demand channel, not this trading sign or its profitability. Prior research on DeFi TVL/market-cap predictability [reports weak evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.00251); this raises the prior probability of rejection.

This is a **retrospective, backfilled historical transfer**, not independently executable OOS. Today's DeFiLlama API has no archived daily release vintages, and historical protocol coverage can change. Binance daily candles do not verify actual order-book fills. Set `independent_executable_oos=false` regardless of historical metrics; full Gate stays closed until a separately preregistered prospective paper test with time-stamped snapshots and observed spreads succeeds.

## Frozen causal rule and net accounting

Each Wednesday at 00:00 UTC identify the most recent completed Sunday `s`. Let `X_s = TVL_s / ETH_close_s`, where `ETH_close_s` is the official spot daily candle ending at Monday 00:00. Compare `X_s` with `X_(s-28 days)`. If `X_s > X_(s-28d)`, target **100% ETH long**; otherwise target **100% USDT cash**. This fixed positive direction is the capital-commitment hypothesis. The Sunday TVL record is given three days of publication/revision latency before Wednesday's decision, and the first order is delayed to **Thursday 00:00 UTC**. This delay is a proxy, not point-in-time proof. No other signal, price trend filter, volatility filter or discretionary skip is allowed. Hold the existing target through Thursday if unchanged; otherwise fully sell or buy at that Thursday daily candle **open**. Force sell any ETH at the **2026-01-01 open** before the end of the account window. Cash before the first eligible Thursday remains cash.

Buy execution is open × `(1+0.0005)` and sell execution is open × `(1-0.0005)`: **5 bp adverse slippage per side**, plus **10 bp taker fee on executed notional per fill**. A buy invests all available USDT after reserving the fee; a sell pays the fee from proceeds. No BNB discount, maker rebate, interest on idle USDT, fractional minimum-lot optimism or external deposits. Record precise quantity, prices, notional and fees for every fill; flag any order smaller than Binance's archived/current 10 USDT notional proxy as an execution failure, without claiming historical lot-size proof. Mark long holdings at each daily close; use every held day's low for conservative drawdown, updating the peak with that day's high **before** its low to avoid a favorable unknown OHLC order. Report gross no-cost and net paths, cost drag, turn count, actual equity, daily dollar PnL and all flat days. If cash/equity becomes nonpositive, seal risk failure.

Two fixed stress paths keep identical Wednesday signals: double adverse slippage to **10 bp per fill**, and one full-day latency (act at Friday 00:00 open rather than Thursday; force exit remains 2026-01-01 open). Each must finish above 10,000 USDT. Do not choose the better schedule after results.
