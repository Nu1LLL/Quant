## Economic mechanism, novelty and evidence grade

[Lyons and Viswanath-Natraj (NBER 2020)](https://www.nber.org/papers/w27136) show that USDT premiums can reflect safe-haven demand while discounts can reflect liquidity and collateral concerns. The **new, unproven transfer hypothesis** is that a recent dislocation in **either** direction marks settlement/risk stress severe enough that holding BTC spot over the next day is worse than holding USDT. A dollar price near the peg allows BTC exposure. The USD value of a USDT cash balance can itself fall in a discount; the account therefore must be marked in USD and cannot treat USDT as exactly one dollar. Coinbase USDT/USD is a venue-specific proxy, not a guaranteed redeemable price or a measure of all stablecoins.

The tracked research already tested USDT/USDC **supply/market-cap liquidity** and a USD-corrected **Coinbase BTC venue premium**. This test isolates the *absolute USDT dollar peg* as a settlement-confidence feature; it never uses Coinbase BTC/USD or a cross-venue BTC price gap. These mechanisms can be statistically correlated. Search of tracked preregistrations and reports found no earlier standalone USDT peg-dislocation rule. The same USDT/USD source was read during the older Coinbase-premium study, and BTC history has been researched extensively, so the signal is **not** a genuinely blind independent OOS. Evidence grade `preregistered_retrospective_current_vintage_coinbase_usdt_peg`; `independent_executable_oos=false`, regardless of numeric success. Coinbase candles are present-day API history with no verified first-publication vintage or historic BBO. The 2024–2026 period is a fixed **retrospective** temporal holdout. No real order is authorized; a full numerical pass could only motivate a separately registered prospective paper study.
