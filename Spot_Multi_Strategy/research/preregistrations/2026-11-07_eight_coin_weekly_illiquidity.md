## Independent mechanism and prior

[Zhang and Li, *Liquidity risk and expected cryptocurrency returns*, first published 3 January 2021](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ijfe.2431), use Amihud illiquidity and find a negative cross-sectional relationship between liquidity and expected returns. The mechanism is compensation for difficult-to-trade assets, economically different from prior rejected weekday demand, realized skewness/lottery preference, jumps, pairs, funding carry and short-horizon order-flow hypotheses. Their historical results do not establish executable Binance perpetual returns after fees, slippage and funding. In particular, the act of harvesting an illiquidity premium may be defeated by the very trading cost it compensates. The precommitted sign is **long high illiquidity, short low illiquidity**.

## Frozen weekly signal, trades and accounting

At Friday `d 00:00 UTC`, use the 28 **completed** UTC days `d-28` through `d-1` only. For each coin and each day `t`, compute `q_t = sum(288 five-minute quote_volume bars)` and `r_t = abs(ln(last five-minute close of t / last five-minute close of t-1))`. All 28 `q_t` must be strictly positive and all inputs finite, else fail the experiment. Define the Amihud score `ILLIQ_d = mean_{t=d-28}^{d-1}(r_t / q_t)`. Sort `(ILLIQ, symbol)` ascending: **short the two lowest scores and long the two highest**; lexical tie breaking. No score cutoff, moving-average gate, ML fit, sign switch, stop, take-profit, Kelly optimization or dynamic leverage.

Use exactly **25% of current equity per leg**: two longs, two shorts, 1.0x gross and zero initial net, below the user's 5x hard maximum. At `d 00:00`, the median of the previous completed day's 288 five-minute quote-volume bars must be at least 50 times each selected leg's USDT notional (forecast participation ≤2%). If any selected leg fails, **all cash for that seven-day week**. Do not use current or future bars to decide. Report ex-post entry/exit fill participation but never filter on it.

For a trading week, enter four legs at Friday `00:05` archived open and exit **exactly seven days later** at next Friday `00:05` archived open. Fully close and reopen even unchanged selections; no intraweek rebalance. Apply **5 bp taker fee and 5 bp adverse slippage per fill** to each leg. Apply every verified actual funding event from entry through just before exit at contemporaneous archived open; long pays positive funding, short receives it. Start with **$10,000**, no external capital or idle yield. Mark account daily at `00:05` using archived open, and inspect all five-minute regular closes plus simultaneous adverse long-low/short-high marks for conservative drawdown and ruin. Fees and funding are assigned to the day in which they occur; final account equity must reconcile executed price PnL, all fees and all funding. Each of the 1,959 days, including cash days, is in daily Sharpe, monthly returns and Monte Carlo.

Run identical signals and capacity decisions under four frozen account scenarios: `base_1x` (5-bp slippage), `double_slippage_1x` (10-bp slippage), `extra_bar_latency_1x` (entry Friday `00:10`, same next-Friday `00:05` exit), and `no_funding_credit_1x` (debits charged, credits suppressed). All stresses must finish above $10,000. No favorable cost or higher-leverage scenario may be substituted.

## Gate and stopping rules

Full-window **net** targets: CAGR ≥40% (40–60% preferred), daily Sharpe >1.5, conservative five-minute maximum drawdown ≤20%, daily dollar-PnL Profit Factor >1.3. At least **150 of the 279** potential weeks must trade. Each of five annual folds must have at least one traded day, positive net return and positive daily Sharpe; terminal 133 days must be profitable. Report every one of 65 calendar months; require ≥39 positive months and a longest losing-month streak ≤3.

Pre-entry BTC market states are the existing fixed bull/bear 30-day log return sign crossed with high/low trailing 30-day sample-volatility versus the prior 90 rolling-volatility median; ties are bear/low. Assign each **daily** account PnL to its day's state, including days inside a held week. Each of four states needs ≥90 formal days and positive aggregate net PnL. Run exactly **5,000 NumPy PCG64 circular 21-day block-bootstrap paths**, seed `20261114`, on all daily account factors and compressed adverse marks. At least 80% must jointly pass the four numerical thresholds; report p05/p50/p95 and ruined/undefined paths.

Any source failure, missing/nonfinite score, capacity logic inconsistency, equity ruin, incomplete fold/state/month, price/fee/funding reconciliation failure, or numeric/stress Gate failure seals this one-shot experiment. Passing the numerical Gate would still leave prospective executable OOS outstanding and permit only a future small-size paper phase, never automatic live orders.
