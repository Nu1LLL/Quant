## Economic hypothesis and evidence boundary

New perpetuals open a short-sale channel to traders who could not cheaply short the underlying. If attention and initial buying pressure temporarily raise the contract price, a **short initiated after the first 30 observed trading minutes** may earn a post-launch correction over the remaining first day. The short-side prediction is an **inference**, not a demonstrated result of the cited papers. [Ante (2019)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3450301) documents exchange-specific listing reactions; [Ante et al. (2021)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10203-021-00323-0) report a negative gross post-listing window in an ICO cross-listing sample; [Augustin, Rubtsov and Shin (2023)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3648406) explain how futures can relax short-sale and arbitrage frictions. None establishes a tradable 30-minute-to-day short on Binance alt perpetuals after fees, funding and launch liquidity.

This *new-contract market-access event* is economically distinct from the already sealed idiosyncratic-volatility, downside-beta, weekday, skewness, illiquidity, funding-spread, lead/lag, flow and jump signals. No event return or sign was consulted to set the rule. The event history is nevertheless reused at program level and the archive has no historical BBO, order queue or actual fills. Evidence grade is **price-blind historical transfer, not independent executable OOS**. Set `independent_executable_oos=false`; even a numerical pass cannot authorize live trading or the full Gate.

## Frozen causal trade rule and $10,000 account

Sort all 215 events by `(t0, contract_symbol)`. Start with **$10,000 cash** on 2021-01-01. At each `entry=t0+30m`, use only the six completed 5m bars `[t0,t0+30m)` to compute the median quote volume. Proposed short notional is **25% of then-current marked account equity**, with gross exposure ≤0.25x and isolated position leverage **1x** (hard cap 5x). Require median quote volume ≥**50× proposed notional**, equivalent to ≤2% of median 5m volume. Otherwise skip to cash and record the cause. No return, momentum, indicator, regime, ML model, coin reputation or post-entry volume enters eligibility. Only **one position at a time**: if an earlier position is still open at a later candidate's entry, skip the later event; equal-time ties use lexical symbol order. No replacement after a skip.

Sell short at the archived `entry` bar **open** with 15 bp adverse slippage and 5 bp taker fee. The scheduled exit is `t0+24h`, using that 5m bar open with 15 bp adverse buy slippage and 5 bp taker fee. At every held bar open from entry through the bar ending immediately before scheduled exit, if its **high** reaches at least **110% of filled entry price**, trigger a protective exit at the **next** 5m bar open; execute the buy at `max(next_bar_open, trigger_bar_high, 1.10×filled_entry_price)` plus 15 bp adverse slippage and fee. A trigger on the final held bar exits at scheduled exit. This is deliberately pessimistic about an intrabar stop; no ideal stop-price fill is assumed. A gap or extreme high can exceed the nominal stop, and account ruin is recorded rather than clipped.

Apply every archived funding event in `[entry, actual_exit)` to the short. To avoid optimistic mark-price reconstruction from trade candles, **all favorable short funding credits are set to zero**, while unfavorable funding debits use the position quantity times `max(entry_filled_price, contemporaneous 5m high)` times the absolute funding rate. This deliberately overcharges negative funding and never claims a positive cash flow from funding. Funding event timestamps and rates must match the frozen source hashes. Charge both fill fees even when an immediate stop occurs. No deposits, Kelly scaling, leverage adjustment, optimizer, take-profit or discretionary override.

Mark open shorts at each available 5m bar close for daily account returns and at each bar **high** for conservative intraday drawdown; cash and fees reconcile exactly. Daily UTC equity is observed after the completed `23:55` bar close, beginning with $10,000 immediately before 2021-01-01 and ending after 2025-12-31. If equity becomes nonpositive, stop and seal a ruined account. Daily UTC account factors span all 1,826 dates including cash days. Daily Sharpe is mean divided by sample standard deviation of those simple factors minus one, multiplied by `sqrt(365)`; CAGR uses the 1,826-day actual period and `365.25` days per year. Profit Factor uses daily **dollar** net gains divided by absolute daily dollar losses. All 60 calendar months are included, even if no trade occurs.
