## Mechanism and evidential limits

The [Federal Reserve's accounting explanation](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/bsd-background-202008.htm) states that, all else equal, a fall in the Treasury General Account (TGA) balance increases banking-system reserves while inflows to TGA drain them. **Frozen prediction:** a recently declining TGA balance favors subsequent BTC spot ownership versus USDT cash through a possible broad risk-liquidity channel. This is a transfer hypothesis, not evidence that bank reserves flow directly into Bitcoin, that all TGA changes are unexpected, or that the relationship is stable. The source is the **fiscal account balance on the Fed liability side**, economically distinct from the Fed's previously preregistered WALCL **asset size** (whose one formal run failed at source acquisition), CPI, on-chain supply, exchange inventory, perpetual turnover and price signals. These macro variables may be statistically correlated; no independence of realized returns is claimed. [Treasury DTS](https://fiscal.treasury.gov/accounting/daily-treasury-statement) defines the operating cash balance. The source's archived daily statements say publication is generally by 4 p.m. Eastern **the following business day**; the fixed one-week observation lag adds a further buffer.

Evidence grade: `preregistered_retrospective_current_vintage_treasury_api`; **`independent_executable_oos=false`**. The API response is a current-vintage historical extraction and may contain retrospective corrections; the program already knows BTC historical market behavior, and the 2024–2025 holdout is retrospective. No historical backtest can create five years of genuinely independent *future* OOS. A future live journal would be required. Daily OHLCV and a 1% volume cap cannot prove fills or true market impact. No live orders.
