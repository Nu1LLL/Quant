## Mechanism, distinctness and evidence

Stablecoin issuance or bridging **onto BSC relative to all chains** may indicate that deployable payment liquidity is concentrating in BNB Chain applications. The frozen transfer prediction is that BSC's rising share of USD-pegged stablecoin tokens precedes stronger BNB spot demand; otherwise cash reduces downside. This **cross-chain allocation** measure differs from the prior aggregate USDT+USDC global issuance rule, which did not measure destination chain or stablecoin migration. It also differs from BNB price trend, perpetual funding/basis, BTC exchange reserves, ETH DeFi TVL/revenue and on-chain transaction fees. It need not be statistically independent: BNB price may drive inflows, bridge accounting can change, and stablecoins can arrive to avoid risk. [DeFiLlama's official free API specification](https://github.com/DefiLlama/api-docs/blob/main/llms-free.txt) exposes chain and all-chain historical stablecoin charts. `totalCirculating.peggedUSD` represents USD-pegged **token counts**, not audited cash reserves, exchange inventory, price-adjusted dollar value or causal investment flows.
