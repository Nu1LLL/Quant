# Preregistration: BTC non-hour quarter-candle drift

**Experiment ID:** `btc_quarter_hour_open_2026_11_08`. This is one new economic hypothesis. This file must be committed and pushed alone before the formal loader opens any price or PnL from the registered rule. No tuning or replacement on this history is permitted after the result is opened.

## Prior and distinction

[Shanaev, Vasenin and Stepanov (2023)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10015199/) report unusually positive **one-minute** BTC returns just after 15-minute candle boundaries in data through 2021, with a short 2022 OOS check. Their explanation is a possible synchronized response of algorithmic trading to newly completed candles, not a proven causal mechanism. The paper's Bitfinex fee-tier backtest is not evidence of Binance USD-M fills or continued profitability in 2024–2026. This registration makes a harder, lower-resolution transfer claim: **the first complete 5-minute interval after a non-hour quarter-hour boundary has enough positive drift to pay fixed Binance perpetual taker costs**. The 5-minute interval can dilute or reverse the one-minute effect; that risk is accepted before reading it. This is not the prior same-weekday cross-sectional seasonality mechanism, funding carry, jump reversal or price/flow lead-lag.

## Frozen decisions and accounting

- At UTC minute **15, 30 and 45 of every hour**, buy BTC at that five-minute candle's **open**; sell at the **next** five-minute candle's open, exactly five minutes later. Thus there are 72 intended nonoverlapping trades per UTC day. There is no price-based signal, forecasting feature, momentum filter, stop, skipped losing day or discretionary holding time. Minute 0 is excluded *before seeing this experiment's returns* because funding settlements can coincide with entry there; no position is held across a UTC hour. Funding cash flow is therefore zero under the frozen minute schedule. Any exchange evidence of non-hour funding settlement is a data-integrity failure, not zero-imputation.
- Use the next open rather than the completed 5-minute candle's close for exit. Entry is an unconditional clock order known in advance; there is no use of the entering bar's future high/low/close to decide whether to trade. Each fill uses **5 bp taker fee plus 1 bp adverse slippage** per side (12 bp approximately per round trip). Buy execution price is open ×1.0001; sell execution price is next open ×0.9999; deduct the 5 bp fee on each executed notional. No rebate, VIP volume discount, assumed market-making queue priority or interest on idle USDT. These fixed cost assumptions may still understate real impact during stress.
- Allocate the entire then-current account cash to the long at each entry, including fees, and return to cash at the exit. No borrowing. Mark account at each held 5-minute bar **low** for adverse drawdown, and at all UTC day boundaries for daily factors. Starting equity is $10,000; if equity ever falls below $100, label practical account ruin, but continue mathematical factor reporting for all dates using log-domain accounting without claiming continued executable trading. Report intended volume, net and gross trade distributions, and the difference between gross and costed results. Profit Factor is positive daily **dollar** PnL divided by the absolute negative daily dollar PnL; if dollar arithmetic underflows after ruin, PF is set to zero and explicitly flagged.

## Frozen Gate and stopping

The **net full-window** Gate requires CAGR ≥40% (40–60% preferred), daily Sharpe >1.5, worst adverse intrabar max drawdown ≤20%, daily-dollar Profit Factor >1.3, and at least 365 traded days. Every full calendar-year forward fold must have positive net return and positive daily Sharpe; 2026's nine-month holdout must also be positive. At least 60% of all 69 months must be strictly profitable, with no more than three consecutive nonpositive months. Each of the 2024, 2025 and 2026 post-paper folds must pass separately. Report all folds and months even if the full account is ruined.

Use four states defined from only the preceding **30 completed UTC BTC daily open-to-open returns**: bullish if their sum >0, bearish otherwise; high volatility if their sample standard deviation ×sqrt(365) ≥80%, low otherwise. State classification begins after 30 warmup days; the first 30 trading days remain in the full account but are not regime-scored. Each state must contain at least 90 classified days and have positive aggregate **net daily dollar PnL**. No state may be chosen after seeing results. Run exactly 5,000 circular 21-day block bootstrap paths, NumPy PCG64 seed `20261119`, from the complete daily net factors and their worst intraday factors. At least 80% of paths must jointly pass CAGR, Sharpe, drawdown and PF; report p05/p50/p95, ruined/undefined counts. Stress the same schedule with **double slippage (2 bp/side)** and **one-bar latency**: entry at the *next 5-minute open* and exit at the following 5-minute open, a fully specified five-minute shift. Both stress paths must finish above $10,000. No double-position overlap is permitted.
