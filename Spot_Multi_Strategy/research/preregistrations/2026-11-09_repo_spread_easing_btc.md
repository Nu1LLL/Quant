## Mechanism, novelty and evidence

The [New York Fed defines SOFR](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/sofr) as a broad Treasury-collateralized overnight borrowing rate and [EFFR](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/effr) as the effective rate on **unsecured** interbank federal-funds transactions. Their difference can proxy a relative squeeze in repo cash financing versus bank funding, though collateral demand, quarter-end balance sheets and monetary operations can also move the spread without changing crypto demand. **Frozen prediction:** when the recent SOFR-minus-EFFR spread declines relative to its preceding ten matched observations, BTC spot ownership during the following week is preferable to USDT cash. A rising or equal spread means cash. This is an unproven transmission hypothesis; a small or inconsistent spread could destroy it.

The full tracked-document screen covered **312** prior preregistrations/sealed reports with SOFR, EFFR, repo spread, repurchase agreement, 回购利率, 融资压力, funding stress and secured overnight. One generic “funding stress” mention in an altcoin realized-skewness liquidity check was the sole match. The source is economically distinct from previous tests of Fed **balance-sheet assets**, Treasury **cash balances**, CPI levels, real yields, credit spreads, derivatives funding rates, on-chain flows, price momentum and calendar timing. Economic distinction does not imply statistically independent returns. The exact rate-spread signal's historical result is unread.

Evidence grade: `preregistered_retrospective_current_vintage_us_money_market_rates`; **`independent_executable_oos=false`**. FRED's downloaded current-vintage history may incorporate corrections; it is not our first-publication archive. The seven-day observation cutoff is conservative about next-business-day publication but cannot prove historical data-vintage identity or executable order-book fills. The research program has seen BTC history. The fixed 2024–2025 split is a historical holdout, not genuinely future OOS. ## Exactly one signal and execution rule

At **every Monday 00:00 UTC** from 2021-01-04 through 2025-12-29, define `cutoff = Monday − 7 calendar days`. Form a sorted intersection of **nonmissing matched SOFR and EFFR observation dates ≤ cutoff**. Take its **last 20** records, subject to most recent date no more than five days before cutoff and span from oldest to newest no more than 40 calendar days. Let `spread_i = SOFR_i − EFFR_i` in percentage points. `recent = arithmetic mean(last 10 spreads)`; `prior = arithmetic mean(previous 10 spreads)`. Target **100% BTC iff `recent < prior`**; equality and increases target **100% USDT cash**. If 20 matched observations or freshness/span checks fail, carry the previous target and place no new order; record the unavailable decision. The date-only preflight found **261 eligible Mondays** (52/52/52/53/52 per 2021–2025), without reading rates. No rate rounding, BTC price, realized volatility, future row, regression coefficient, ML model, manual override or news signal enters the decision.

Execute a changed target at that Monday's Binance spot daily **open**. For the one-day-latency stress, execute the same frozen Monday decision at Tuesday's open, with no recomputation. Force-sell remaining BTC at **2026-01-01 00:00 UTC open**, assigning the sale proceeds/cost to the 2025-12-31 daily factor. Each fill pays **10 bp taker fee plus 5 bp adverse slippage**: buy at open×1.0005, sell at open×0.9995. Round BTC down to 0.00001, keep USDT dust, require ≥10 USDT notional, positive equity and cash. Buy notional must be ≤1% of the **preceding 30 completed daily bars' median quote volume**. Failure of capacity, cash, account reconciliation or gross ≤1× check seals rather than skipping.

Mark BTC at daily close; conservative intraday drawdown assumes high before low on every held day, including fills. Reconcile daily account factors, fills, fees and terminal cash. Report same-cost BTC buy-and-hold, zero-cost same-signal, doubled-slippage (10 bp/side, same fee) and one-day-delayed scenarios; both stress endpoints must exceed 10,000 USDT.
