## Independent economic rationale and prior uncertainty

[Jia, Liu and Yan (2021)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612320303135) construct intraday five-minute higher moments and report a **negative cross-sectional relation between realized skewness and next-day crypto returns** in a 2017–2019 Bitfinex sample. The proposed mechanism is lottery-like demand for coins with an extreme upside tail, followed by lower next-day expected returns. This is a distribution-shape/lottery-demand hypothesis, distinct from the rejected same-weekday seasonal-demand test, BTC time-series jump reversal, cross-coin lead-lag, funding carry and pair-distance hypotheses. The standardized realized-skewness definition follows [Amaya et al. (2015), documented in the highfrequency package](https://search.r-project.org/CRAN/refmans/highfrequency/html/rSkew.html). A separate [2021 MAX study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40854-021-00291-9) reports skewness is not independently predictive after controls, so the prior for a durable effect is mixed. Neither paper provides proof of a Binance USD-M strategy profitable after our full trading costs. No MAX, volatility or kurtosis signal is tested in this experiment.

## Frozen signal and daily positions

For each coin (i) and UTC entry day (d), use only the **288 completed five-minute bars of the immediately preceding UTC day (d-1)**. For each such bar (j), compute (r_{i,j}=\ln(C_{i,j}/C_{i,j-1})) from archived closes; the first return uses the last bar close of day (d-2). All 288 closes are known at `d 00:00 UTC`; no current held-period price enters the signal. Set

\[RSK_{i,d-1}=\frac{\sqrt{288}\sum_{j=1}^{288}r_{i,j}^{3}}{\left(\sum_{j=1}^{288}r_{i,j}^{2}\right)^{3/2}}.\]

Any zero/nonfinite denominator or score fails the full run. Sort `(RSK, symbol)` ascending. **Long the two lowest-skewness coins; short the two highest-skewness coins**, with lexical ties. Each leg is 25% of current account equity: 50% long, 50% short, **1.0x gross, zero initial net**, below the user's 5x hard cap. No forecast model, score cutoff, moving average, adaptive sign, Kelly fraction, stop, take-profit or leverage optimizer. Repeat the rank daily, closing and reopening every leg even if unchanged.

At decision time `d 00:00`, for each selected coin the trailing 288 completed five-minute **quote-volume median** must be at least 50 times that leg's USDT notional (forecast participation at most 2%). If any fails, hold all cash for the day. Use the baseline account's prior equity to fix this decision and replay the identical selected-day schedule under every cost/funding stress; stress scenarios size their own legs from their own prior equity. Actual entry/exit bar participation is reported only as an ex-post execution diagnostic and cannot filter a trade.

Enter all legs at `d 00:05` archived open, exit at `(d+1) 00:05` archived open, exactly 24 hours later. Charge each long/short leg **5 bps taker fee plus 5 bps adverse slippage per fill**, with no maker rebate; total fully traded account roundtrip is about 20 bps before funding. Charge or credit each actual verified funding event after entry and before exit at its contemporaneous archived open: long pays positive funding, short receives it. Starting account **$10,000**, no external cash, idle yield or off-account compounding. Track every five-minute regular close and conservative simultaneous long-low/short-high adverse mark; any nonpositive account equity or missing price/funding data seals failure. Final account equity must reconcile executed price PnL, fees and funding.

Run four frozen scenarios with the **same signals and capacity schedule**: `base_1x` (5-bp slippage), `double_slippage_1x` (10-bp slippage), `extra_bar_latency_1x` (entry at `d 00:10`, same next-day exit) and `no_funding_credit_1x` (funding debits charged, favorable per-leg credits suppressed). The three stresses must each end above $10,000. No alternative scenario or higher leverage is promoted after viewing results.
