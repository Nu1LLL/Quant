## Mechanism and prior evidence

Changes in the circulating supply of established fiat-backed USD stablecoins can proxy new dollar liquidity entering or leaving crypto markets. **Prediction:** if combined USDT+USDC token supply grew over the preceding 28 completed UTC days, an equal-entry-weight basket of BTC, ETH and BNB spot earns positive subsequent risk-adjusted returns; otherwise holding USDT cash protects capital. This *outside capital / payment-rail liquidity* source is separate from previously tested native PoW coin issuance, BTC miner margins, DeFi protocol revenue or TVL, wallet-exchange reserves, price trend, funding, market microstructure and Wikipedia attention. Supply may expand because users seek stablecoins rather than risky coins, and prices may move *before* stablecoin creation. The [stablecoin issuance event study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612320316810) finds a temporal association but not this four-week gate or executable profits; a [crypto-stablecoin causality study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40854-022-00343-8) cautions against treating supply as exogenous. Neither paper's result is independent OOS for this rule.

The free [DeFiLlama stablecoin chart API](https://github.com/DefiLlama/api-docs/blob/main/llms-free.txt) provides current reconstructed historical circulation, not verifiable daily first-published vintages or future-proof reserve quality. `totalCirculating.peggedUSD` is **token count** for USD-pegged coins, not cash deposits, deployable exchange balances, or price-adjusted USD value; chain migration, bridge accounting, redemption, source revisions and temporary depegs can distort the proxy.
