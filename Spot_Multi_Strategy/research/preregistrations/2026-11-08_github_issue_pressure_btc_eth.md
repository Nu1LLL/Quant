## Economic idea, independence and evidence

Unexpected public issue creation in the core protocol/client codebase may signal a project-specific reliability or maintenance burden. If it is large relative to that repository's own recent baseline, capital may rotate away from its coin over the following week. The fixed prediction is **short the coin with unusually higher issue pressure, long the other**. All created issues are counted; they are not claimed to be verified bugs or security incidents. [Trockman et al.
