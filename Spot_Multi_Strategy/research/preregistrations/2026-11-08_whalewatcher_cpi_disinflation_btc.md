## Economic mechanism and limits

The fixed prediction is that **decelerating published U.S. headline year-over-year CPI** eases macro pressure on a risky crypto asset, making subsequent unleveraged BTC spot ownership preferable to cash. Accelerating or unchanged CPI means cash. This is a **monthly realized inflation-state** rule, economically separate from the program's previous Fed balance-sheet liquidity, Bitcoin on-chain supply, exchange inventory, funding, futures turnover, price trend and event-calendar tests. It is not a CPI-surprise rule: there is no contemporaneous survey consensus, and it cannot claim to predict the immediate release jump. [Pinchuk (2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.10117) reports a negative *announcement-window* BTC reaction to positive inflation surprises; that finding neither establishes nor tests this month-long CPI-state rule. The relation could change across inflation and monetary-policy regimes.
