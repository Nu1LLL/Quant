## Mechanism and evidence grade

The [Cboe VIX history page](https://www.cboe.com/tradable_products/vix/vix_historical_data) describes VIX as 30-day option-implied U.S. equity volatility. VIX3M is the corresponding longer-horizon volatility index. When spot VIX exceeds VIX3M, short-horizon crash protection is unusually dear (backwardation), which may transmit a risk-off shock into BTC; ordinary contango may favor risk-taking. [IMF research](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/001/2023/213/article-A001-en.xml) documents time-varying crypto/financial-market spillovers. [Luo et al.](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6233752) find a contemporaneous VIX-slope/BTC association, **not** proof that the frozen weekly rule below predicts returns. The transfer prediction is **long BTC in contango, short BTC in backwardation**. This external equity-options risk-price source is economically distinct from the program's previously tested BTC price trend, crypto DVOL, Fed/Treasury balance-sheet levels, real rates, on-chain flow, miner economics, futures positioning and Wikipedia attention.
