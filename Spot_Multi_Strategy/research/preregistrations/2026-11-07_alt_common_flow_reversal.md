## Economic mechanism and fixed universe

[Makarov and Schoar (2020), *Trading and Arbitrage in Cryptocurrency Markets*](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/7f91bfb5-ba77-4d0e-9c79-ec75e104e6cc) report that part of the immediate effect of **common signed trading volume** in Bitcoin reversed over subsequent five-minute periods. Their common factor was measured **across exchanges for Bitcoin**, not across assets or on 2021–2025 Binance perpetuals. This is therefore a new, narrower transfer hypothesis: simultaneous aggressive flow bursts in three liquid altcoin perpetuals create temporary common price pressure that reverses over the next 30 minutes after realistic fees. It is not the previously rejected single-BTC price-jump reversal or BTC/ETH relative-distance trade. The literature result is motivation, not proof of this strategy's alpha.

Fix exactly three Binance USD-M perpetuals, **XRPUSDT, ADAUSDT, DOGEUSDT**, in that lexical order. All three enter and exit together in the opposite direction to the common flow signal. No after-result coin ranking, omissions, substitution, sign flip, or venue search. The user-requested altcoin expansion and these three contracts' pre-OOS archive availability determine the universe, not inspected returns. No BTC/ETH position, WhaleWatcher input, AI advice, or external news enters this experiment.

## Causal five-minute signal and fills

1. Partition each symbol's validated minute bars into nonoverlapping UTC 5-minute windows `[W-5min,W)`, where `W` is on the five-minute grid. For each completed window, sum Binance `quote_volume` as `Q_i(W)` and `taker_buy_quote_volume` as `B_i(W)`. If `Q=0`, no alert. Set signed aggressive-quote imbalance `OI_i(W)=2B_i(W)/Q_i(W)-1` in `[-1,1]` (a 1m K-line aggregate proxy, **not** L2 order-book imbalance). Compute baseline `M_i(W)` as the arithmetic mean of **exactly the 12 immediately preceding completed five-minute windows'** quote volumes, excluding the current window. If any baseline is zero/nonfinite, no alert. Thus only the past 60 minutes and current completed five-minute window enter a decision.
2. For each `W` within the fixed OOS, count coins with both `Q_i(W)/M_i(W) >= 2.0` and `OI_i(W) >= +0.20`; also count coins with `Q_i/M_i >=2.0` and `OI_i <= -0.20`. If **at least two** of the three satisfy the positive condition and the median of all three `OI_i(W)` is positive, signal **short all three**. If at least two satisfy the negative condition and the median is negative, signal **long all three**. A signal cannot meet both sides. There is no price-jump, momentum, chart or discretionary filter. Thresholds are fixed ex ante; do not optimize them on 2021–2025.
3. An alert at completed window end `W` is formed after that window closes. The earliest reference fill is the **minute open at `W+1min`**; all three legs receive adverse execution prices. Fix the exit at that entry timestamp plus **30 minutes**, using the then-observed minute open and the same adverse price rule. No earlier profit target, stop, reversal or extension. Ignore alerts while a basket is open/pending; a new signal is eligible only when its proposed entry fill is **at or after `last_entry_fill+60min`**. This cooldown is fixed and does not depend on PnL. A signal whose entry or scheduled exit would be at/after `2026-01-01 00:00` is ignored. No overnight or annual reset of an open trade; its marked daily return belongs to the day in which it occurs.
4. The whole entry minute's low (long) or high (short) is included in minute-pressure drawdown. The exit minute OHLC is excluded because exit is at that minute's open. A pending trade is never canceled based on price/flow seen before its scheduled fill. The three legs' position quantities are computed simultaneously from the same pre-entry marked equity; accounting order does not change them.

## Account and net accounting

- Start with **10,000 USDT**. Fixed formal portfolio: **2x total gross entry notional**, `2*E_pre/3` executed USDT notional in each coin. Fixed 1x signal-quality comparator: `E_pre/3` each. At each simultaneous entry, calculate signed quantity using each leg's own adverse executed proxy price, then deduct the fee; hold quantities fixed until exit. Never select between the two based on their returns or try 3–5x to rescue Sharpe. If any formal or stress scenario has marked equity <=0, seal a failed candidate; no partial-path performance promotion.
- Each entry and exit leg pays **5 bp taker fee** on executed notional and an additional **25 bp adverse reference-price adjustment** (15 bp spread/slippage plus 10 bp impact/latency), additive: buy `p_ref*(1+0.0025)`, sell `p_ref*(1-0.0025)`. Fixed cost stress doubles only the nonfee adverse component to 50 bp, with fee still 5 bp. These are assumed penalties, not measured order-book execution. Initial-equity flat-price roundtrip cost is approximately 60 bp at 1x, 120 bp at 2x.
- For each actual funding `calc_time` in a leg's half-open `[entry_fill_time,exit_fill_time)`, long pays positive signed rate and short receives it, using fixed quantity times the most recent completed 1m trade close as notional proxy. A minute bar `[t-1min,t)` has archived `close_time=t-1ms`; funding at `t+0...47ms` uses that bar, never `[t,t+1min)`. No-beneficial-funding-credit stress sets positive funding cash flows to zero and preserves charges. The proxy is not Binance's true mark price.
- Mark open positions every minute to each coin's minute close. For pressure drawdown use each long leg's low or short leg's high **simultaneously**, even if actual extrema occurred at different seconds. At each held minute, first update the running peak with the regular minute-close equity, then compare that minute's adverse mark to the updated peak. Daily equity at UTC midnight is recorded **before** funding stamped at that boundary; events at midnight or milliseconds after belong to the new UTC day. Monthly/annual returns use the same boundary convention.

## Frozen Gate and stop rule

1. **Data/cause:** all 192 K-line and 180 funding archives pass the frozen double-hash, full-grid and finite-value checks; all features precede signal and fills follow the one-minute lag. If not, report data-integrity failure with no accepted partial PnL.
2. **1x signal prerequisite:** net five-year CAGR >0, daily Sharpe >1.5, daily dollar-PnL PF >1.3, and minute-pressure max drawdown <=20%. If this fails, 2x results are diagnostic only and cannot qualify.
3. **2x formal numbers:** net five-year CAGR >=40% (report whether 40–60% band), daily Sharpe >1.5, minute-pressure max drawdown <=20%, and daily dollar-PnL PF >1.3. `CAGR=(E_end/10000)^(365.25/1826)-1`; daily Sharpe uses `sqrt(365)*mean(daily_return)/sample_sd(daily_return)` with zero risk-free rate; PF is sum positive daily dollar PnL divided by absolute sum negative daily dollar PnL. Zero-trade/no-loss cases give insufficient evidence. Include all flat days, all 60 monthly returns, positive/negative/flat month counts, worst month, longest losing-month streak, completed basket count and all costs.
4. **Walk-forward:** each of 2021–2025 must have at least one entered basket, positive marked net calendar-year return and positive daily Sharpe with the same frozen rule. Cross-year positions allocate daily marked PnL to the correct day.
5. **Four market states:** at each entry signal time `W`, calculate the equal-weight mean of the three individual **90-day log close returns** ending at `W` (>=0 bull, otherwise bear). Calculate sample SD of the 720 hourly equal-weight mean log returns ending at `W`, and compare it with the median of **60 UTC-daily snapshots** at times `D-60d,...,D-1d` where `D=floor_UTC_day(W)` and each snapshot uses its trailing 720 hourly equal-weight returns. Current SD >= median is high, else low. Any missing required close or snapshot is insufficient state evidence. Allocate complete cost-net basket trade PnL to its entry bucket; require at least one trade and positive summed net PnL in each bull/bear × high/low bucket, with no unknown bucket.
6. **21-day block Monte Carlo:** for exactly 5,000 paths, use one `numpy.random.Generator(numpy.random.PCG64(20261109))` stream. Each path independently draws 87 uniform starts in `0,...,1825`; take 21 consecutive daily records circularly from each, concatenate and truncate to 1,826. Store each day's ending regular-equity factor `a`, maximum regular-equity factor `b` including day start, minimum adverse-equity factor `c` including day start, and within-day maximum drawdown `d` of adverse equity from the prior regular peak (updating with the same minute's regular close first). Rebase sampled days on their new start; exact path max minute-pressure drawdown is the worse of `d` and `1-start_equity*c/prior_global_regular_peak` each day, updating global peak with `b` afterwards. Verify compression reproduces the original formal-minute drawdown before accepting Monte Carlo. Recompute CAGR, daily Sharpe and daily dollar PF for each path; require **>=80%** jointly pass all four formal numerical thresholds. Report p05/p50/p95 and undefined counts. Both doubled-nonfee-cost and no-beneficial-funding-credit 2x stresses must end above 10,000 USDT.
7. **Evidence:** even a numerical pass remains below the full independent executable-data Gate unless point-in-time quote depth, contemporaneous archive availability and actual three-leg fills are independently replayable. There is no such five-year BBO/L2/fill evidence here. The source paper predates OOS but studies a different market factor; this is a fixed **internal mechanism-level historical holdout**, not a verified prospective or executable OOS. No automatic live trade is authorized; any genuinely complete pass enters only small-position paper trading.
