## Mechanism and evidence boundary

[Hoang and Baur, *Loaded for Bear*](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3902504) report that BTC transfers onto exchanges are associated with increased selling pressure and lower subsequent returns, while withdrawals suggest reduced selling pressure. The fixed transfer prediction here is that a **28-day decline** in BTC held by identified exchange addresses will favor BTC spot ownership over cash in the following week. The level of exchange reserves is economically distinct from the program's previously tested BTC age/spend, fees, difficulty, MVRV, active addresses, Binance futures open interest, stablecoin issuance, DeFi TVL, venue premium, seasonal and price factors. The prior prospective exchange-supply journal has not tested this trading rule.

[Coin Metrics explicitly provides a different point-in-time family](https://docs.coinmetrics.io/network-data/network-data-overview/exchange/point-in-time-flows) to avoid this look-ahead; `SplyExPitNtv` is not available on the Community endpoint (403 in a price-blind access check). At each Wednesday 00:00 UTC decision, let `s` be the preceding Sunday. Read the published daily native BTC exchange supply at `s` and `s−28 days`. A Coin Metrics day label is treated conservatively as available no earlier than the next UTC day, and the Wednesday decision leaves additional time for publication; the next order occurs **Thursday 00:00 UTC** at Binance daily open. This latency is still only a proxy because historical first-published metric vintages are unavailable. Hold an unchanged target without turnover. No price, volatility, MVRV, funding, TVL, Wikipedia, trend or market-state variable enters the decision. Start in cash; fully sell any remaining BTC at the **2026-01-01 open**. Attribute terminal proceeds/fee to the 2025-12-31 daily end equity so all 1,826 factors exactly reconcile.

For every buy, execute at `open ×1.0005`; sell at `open ×0.9995` (**5 bp adverse slippage each fill**) and pay **10 bp taker fee per executed notional**. Invest cash after reserving fee, round BTC quantity **down** to `0.00001 BTC`, keep residual USDT, and require each executed fill ≥10 USDT. Before buying, require proposed notional ≤**1% of the previous 30 completed UTC days' median Binance quote volume**; a failure is an execution Gate failure, not a skipped trade. This is a coarse capacity proxy, not historical order-book proof. Cash pays zero interest; no maker rebate, BNB discount, tax or external deposit. On each held day mark at Binance close and compute conservative intraday drawdown with its **high before its low**; entry/exit days include pre-fill and post-fill equity. Equity ≤0 fails.
