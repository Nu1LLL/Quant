If any source or Gate component fails, seal the experiment; do not reverse its sign, alter its window, choose another coin/page, delete a year, change a cost, or increase leverage.

## Mechanism and evidence grade

Attention may lead short-lived crypto demand. [Liu and Tsyvinski](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24877) report that investor-attention proxies forecast crypto returns, while [ElBahrawy et al.](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/blockchain/articles/10.3389/fbloc.2019.00012/full) study Wikipedia attention and cryptocurrencies. This **new transfer hypothesis** is narrower: the coin whose English Wikipedia attention has accelerated more relative to its own baseline will outperform the other coin over the next calendar month. The sign is fixed as long high-attention, short low-attention. Attention can instead react to prior price moves; the papers do not establish this specific executable spread. This source is economically distinct from previously tested on-chain supply, miner stress, venue premium, funding, price momentum, and calendar signals. No price, return, funding, volatility, trend, or other input enters selection.

Evidence grade: `preregistered_retrospective_current_vintage_wikimedia_attention`; `signal_specific_untouched_2020_2025_holdout=true`, `independent_executable_oos=false`. The historical view series is a current API vintage, not a saved set of first-published vintages; trading cannot be shown to have occurred at historical prices. A three-day publication lag is a conservative timing rule, not proof of vintage fidelity. Full Gate cannot pass until a separately preregistered prospective paper sample supplies genuine independent OOS and executable fill evidence.

## Frozen decision, execution and stop

At **00:00 UTC on the first Thursday of each month** from **2020-02 through 2025-12**, use views only through the **preceding Sunday 23:59 UTC**. January 2020 is cash because the official 2019-12 futures 1d K-line warmup archives returned 404 in the pre-result source audit, so a prior-30-day capacity screen is unavailable; this is frozen before reading returns. For each article, calculate `recent = mean(daily views on the last 28 complete days ending Sunday)` and `baseline = mean(daily views on the 84 complete days immediately before those 28)`. Require both means >0. Define attention acceleration `A = recent / baseline`. If `A_BTC > A_ETH`, target BTC long and ETH short; if `A_ETH > A_BTC`, target ETH long and BTC short; exact equality means cash until the next monthly decision. This is a fixed relative-attention signal; no significance threshold or return-based filter.

Execute at that Thursday's Binance futures 1d open, taking **+1.0× current account equity notional** on the long and **−1.0× equity notional** on the short (2.0× gross, 0 net); round quantities down to each contract's fixed research precision of 0.001 BTC and 0.001 ETH and leave unused equity as cash collateral. At each next monthly decision close and reset both legs to the new target, including if the ranking is unchanged, so turnover is not understated. Buy fills at open ×1.0005, sell fills at open ×0.9995, plus **10 bp taker fee** on every filled notional per leg. Debit actual archived funding rates to signed positions at each exchange event (`−signed_quantity × same-hour-mark-open × funding_rate`); missing or malformed funding or mark data fails the experiment. A separate stress doubles slippage to 10 bp per fill, another executes each fixed signal at Friday 00:00 UTC open without recomputing it. Both must retain positive terminal equity. Include forced exit and fees at 2026-01-01 open in the final 2025-12-31 account factor. All buys, sells, and funding debit/credit must reconcile exactly to cash plus marked positions.

Cap **initial gross exposure at 2.0× and intraday gross/equity at 2.5×**. If the previous day's close gross/equity exceeds 2.5, liquidate both legs at the next daily open with the same cost and remain cash until the next scheduled monthly decision. If equity falls to or below 0.5% of current gross notional under a conservative same-day long-low/short-high mark, declare liquidation/ruin and Gate failure; do not restart the account. A missing bar, invalid price/rate/quantity, negative equity, or non-reconciling ledger seals failure. Mark daily equity at futures close; conservative drawdown uses long low and short high on the same day (a deliberately adverse bound). Require each entry/adjustment to be ≤1% of the prior 30 completed daily quote-volume median for its contract. The first possible February 2020 decision has 30 complete January/February bars of warmup; no 2019-12 volume is used. If unavailable or exceeded, seal capacity failure. No live orders.

## Unified Gate and stop rule

Net full-account CAGR ≥40% (report whether within preferred 40–60%), daily Sharpe >1.5 (`sqrt(365)`, zero risk-free), conservative maximum drawdown ≤20%, and daily-dollar profit factor >1.3. Require ≥24 monthly entries and ≥36/72 strictly profitable months, at most three consecutive nonpositive months, and report worst month. Every calendar-year 2020–2025 fold must have at least one trade, positive net return and positive Sharpe; the fixed-rule 2024–2025 retrospective holdout must independently satisfy all four numeric thresholds. The folds test repeated application of a frozen rule, not trained walk-forward optimization.

For regime audit only, label each day using **prior** 31 completed BTC daily closes: positive 30-day log return = bull, otherwise bear; sample standard deviation of the 30 daily log returns ×`sqrt(365)` ≥80% = high volatility. All four bull/bear × high/low cells must contain ≥90 days and positive account-dollar PnL. Run exactly **5,000** circular **21-day** block-bootstrap paths of net account factors and synchronized conservative intraday marks using NumPy PCG64 seed **20261115**; require ≥80% of paths to jointly pass the four numeric thresholds, and report p05/p50/p95, ruined and undefined paths. Any data, execution, funding, capacity, numeric, annual, holdout, monthly, regime, Monte Carlo, stress, or independent-OOS failure rejects the experiment. No parameter update or second formal run on this history. A positive historical outcome would only justify a new, prospective paper study.
