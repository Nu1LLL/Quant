## Economic mechanism and evidence grade

Binance defines its [top-trader account long/short ratio](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Top-Trader-Long-Short-Ratio-Accounts) as the net-long versus net-short account count among the 20% of users with the highest margin balance; its [global account ratio](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Long-Short-Ratio) covers all positioned accounts. The fixed **untested** prediction is that unusually bullish top-trader account positioning relative to all-account positioning precedes positive BTC returns over the next month, and the reverse precedes negative returns. Both fields measure accounts, so their ratio has a coherent denominator; neither is the top-trader **position-size** ratio. This is a *participant-composition* mechanism distinct from the program's previously tested aggregate open-interest level/change, funding carry, price trend, fees, miner stress, attention, and on-chain supply. Top traders need not be informed, and the ratio can be a lagging sentiment measure; the sign is not guaranteed. No other feature enters trading decisions.

Evidence grade `preregistered_retrospective_current_vintage_binance_positioning`; `signal_specific_2021_2025_first_holdout=true`; **`independent_executable_oos=false`**. Historical archive files are current vendor vintages, not a preserved record of exactly when each statistic was first published; the 48-hour delay is a latency proxy.
