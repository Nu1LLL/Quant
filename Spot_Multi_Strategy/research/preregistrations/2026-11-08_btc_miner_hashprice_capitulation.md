## Economic mechanism and evidence limit

Miners face dollar revenue from block issuance plus transaction fees against energy and capital costs. A persistently low revenue per unit of network hash rate, accompanied by declining hash rate, may reflect stress and forced exit. Our **untested transfer prediction** is that after such capitulation, future spot BTC demand exceeds near-term miner sale pressure. This is a *joint miner margin and exit* mechanism; it is distinct from the program's prior standalone BTC fee demand, difficulty-recovery event, exchange-wallet inventory, futures open interest, old-coin spend, market cap/realized cap, active addresses, and price-only hypotheses. ## Frozen weekly rule and net account

At Wednesday 00:00 UTC, let `s` be the preceding Sunday. Compute daily `R_d` for exactly `s−364` through `s` (365 days). Let `m28` be the arithmetic mean of `R` over `s−27..s`, and `median365` the median of all 365 daily `R` values. Let `h_recent` be the arithmetic mean of `HashRate` over `s−27..s`, and `h_prior` the mean over `s−55..s−28`. **Target 100% BTC long iff `m28 < median365` AND `h_recent < h_prior`; otherwise target 100% USDT cash.** Equality means cash. No BTC price trend, fee alone, exchange supply, funding, market regime or prior trade PnL enters the decision. The next order executes Thursday 00:00 UTC at Binance spot daily open. An unchanged target incurs no fill. Start in cash, forcibly sell any remaining BTC at 2026-01-01 00:00 open, assigning its proceeds/cost to the 2025-12-31 daily factor.

Each buy uses `open ×1.0005`, each sell `open ×0.9995` (5 bp adverse slippage) and pays 10 bp taker fee per executed notional. Invest cash after reserving fee; BTC quantity rounded **down** to `0.00001`, residual cash retained; require every fill ≥10 USDT. Before buying, notional must be ≤1% of the previous 30 **completed** UTC days' median Binance quote volume; failure seals execution Gate, never skips the prescribed order. Cash pays zero interest.
