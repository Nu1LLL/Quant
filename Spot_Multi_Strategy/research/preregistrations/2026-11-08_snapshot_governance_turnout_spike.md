# Preregistration: Snapshot governance turnout surprise in crypto tokens

Registered before fetching any proposal vote counts, Binance price values, or strategy returns. This is **one** new economic hypothesis: unusually high participation in a token's governance vote may signal sustained holder attention and subsequent token demand. It is distinct from price trend, funding, on-chain fees, TVL, token supply and macro releases. The previously tested Wikipedia and GitHub attention studies are not this source; correlation or incremental information is unproven and must be reported, not assumed.

## Fixed universe, sources, timing and evidence

- Five fixed Binance spot USDT pairs and Snapshot spaces: `UNIUSDT` / `uniswapgovernance.eth`, `COMPUSDT` / `comp-vote.eth`, `SUSHIUSDT` / `sushigov.eth`, `1INCHUSDT` / `1inch.eth`, `AAVEUSDT` / `aavedao.eth`. `BTCUSDT` is for regime and passive comparison only. All five were in the already frozen 2020-12-31 crypto universe and were spot-trading in Binance public `exchangeInfo` on registration day. Snapshot's public GraphQL endpoint returned a proposal timestamp before 2021 for UNI, SUSHI and AAVE, and before July 2021 for COMP and 1INCH; no participation values or price outcomes were opened. No asset replacement or selection by this experiment's returns is allowed.
- Fixed accounting sample: 2021-01-01 00:00 through 2026-01-01 00:00 UTC, five complete years. Snapshot proposals ending from 2020-01-01 onward may warm up each space's prior-six-proposal median. Development years 2021–2023; historical holdout 2024–2025. Annual walk-forward checks all five calendar years with the unchanged rule. No fit to returns and no parameter optimization.
- Use [Snapshot's official Hub GraphQL API](https://github.com/snapshot-labs/snapshot-docs/blob/master/tools/api/README.md) proposal `id, space.id, created, start, end, votes, scores_updated` from the five exact spaces, paginated to exhaustion in ascending creation order. Duplicate IDs, malformed timestamps, noninteger/negative votes, proposal end before start, or a nonreproducible pagination digest make the **whole experiment a data failure**. Store a small normalized, hashed event manifest; do not store proposal prose or individual voter identities. A proposal with `end` outside the accounting period can only be warmup, never a trade. No deleted proposal can be reconstructed from today's API; this is a known survivorship risk.
- Trade prices use only official Binance Vision monthly spot `1d` OHLC archives, validated against each `.CHECKSUM`, for all six fixed symbols over the entire five-year accounting window; BTC needs 2020-10 through 2020-12 warmup for regime labels. Reject a missing month, timestamp, duplicate, invalid OHLC, or source hash mismatch for **any** fixed symbol; do not delete that symbol/year or substitute another data feed. Original ZIPs are streamed and discarded, not kept on disk. Daily lows bound adverse intraday path for held spot positions; no claim of executable order-book fills.
- Historical Snapshot final vote count is not an archived end+24-hour vintage. Its exact value and later edits may have been unavailable at the assumed decision time. Therefore evidence grade is permanently `preregistered_retrospective_historical_holdout`; `independent_oos=false` regardless of performance. A numerical pass cannot satisfy the full Gate or authorize trading. A separate prospective capture would be needed before true independent OOS can be claimed.

## Frozen signal and execution

1. For each proposal that has ended, compare its final `votes` count to the median vote count of the **six most recent proposals of the same space whose ends are strictly earlier**, including all valid warmup proposals. A signal requires six predecessors, at least 20 votes, and current votes at least twice the median. A zero median is permitted; the 20-vote floor still applies. Simultaneous proposal ends do not enter one another's history. No title/body, vote direction, proposal outcome, price, or future governance event affects the signal.
2. The assumed earliest decision time is `proposal end + 24 hours`, and the entry is the **first UTC daily open strictly after** that time. If the decision is at or after the sample end, no trade. If `scores_updated` exists and is after the assumed decision time, that proposal is source-ineligible and its vote count cannot update the six-proposal history; report the count of such exclusions. If this retrospective availability check removes every eligible signal or cannot be interpreted reproducibly, seal as source failure, not a successful zero-trade result.
3. Long the matching spot token, hold exactly seven UTC days, then sell at that day's open. Ignore further signals for a token while its position is open. If multiple proposals for a token yield the same entry open, enter at most once. No shorting, options, perpetuals or funding; no leverage. At each entry, spend at most 20% of then-current marked account equity and at most available cash. Five simultaneous positions can use at most the whole cash balance. Initial capital is 10,000 USDT and idle cash earns zero.
4. Charge each entry and exit 10 basis points commission **plus** 10 basis points adverse spread/slippage, based on executed notional (40 bps round trip before price movement). Entry execution is open ×1.001; exit execution is open ×0.999, with fee separately deducted. Reject nonpositive cash/equity, unfillable positions and arithmetic errors rather than assuming a better fill. Daily equity and worst-case same-day low equity include all open positions and costs. No margin liquidation model is needed for unleveraged spot.

## Frozen comparison and Gate

- Report net CAGR, daily-return Sharpe (365-day annualization), worst daily-low drawdown, daily-return profit factor, calendar-year returns, positive months out of 60, trade count, median holding period, turnover, net performance by asset and by regime. The passive comparison is the five fixed spot tokens at 20% initial capital each, held for the same five years with the same entry/exit cost assumptions; it does not change the strategy gate.
- Gate: net CAGR at least 40% (target range 40–60%), Sharpe >1.5, worst-path maximum drawdown no worse than −20%, daily-return profit factor >1.3; each annual walk-forward year must be net positive; historical holdout 2024–2025 must independently satisfy numerical thresholds; positive months at least 36/60, with no run of four consecutive negative months. Classify each day from BTC's **previous 90-day** close return: bull >+20%, bear <−20%, otherwise sideways; each regime must have at least 90 daily observations and positive net return. Fees/slippage are mandatory. Zero-trade or undefined statistics fail.
- Run 5,000 moving 30-day block bootstrap paths of the complete net daily return series, fixed seed 20261108. The joint fraction meeting CAGR≥40%, Sharpe>1.5, drawdown≤20% and PF>1.3 must be at least 95%. Report its actual value and 5th/50th/95th percentiles. No resampling of selected trades alone.
- **Full Gate must fail on independent OOS** for this retrospective study even if all numerical gates pass. No live or paper order is authorized by this registration. Stop after one complete formal run; if data fail, seal that result. Do not retune turnout threshold, holding time, cost, universe, dates, leverage or source availability rules on the same history. Any future strategy combination needs its own separate preregistration and untouched OOS.
