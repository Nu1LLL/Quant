## Mechanism, distinctness and evidence grade

Ethereum users pay ETH for block space. Since [EIP-1559](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1559), the base portion of fees is burned, though the Coin Metrics total-fee metric also includes non-burned payments. A rising amount of ETH paid **per confirmed transaction** may reflect stronger willingness to pay for Ethereum execution and potential subsequent ETH demand. The frozen directional prediction is **higher recent fee burden than the prior period favors ETH spot over cash**. This is a block-space **usage-price** measure, distinct from the earlier BTC miner fee level, BTC mempool backlog, ETH ledger supply contraction, ETH DeFi TVL, Lido stake, price, funding and spot-flow rules. It may correlate with those variables or simply mark temporary NFT/DeFi congestion; transaction complexity and changing block capacity also affect fee per transaction. It does not directly measure future investment flows or the base fee burned. Coin Metrics lists [`FeeTotNtv` and the fees taxonomy](https://docs.coinmetrics.io/network-data/network-data-overview/fees-and-revenue); [Coin Metrics' timestamp convention](https://docs.coinmetrics.io/service-and-support/faqs) assigns aggregated network metrics to the start of the measured day, so the decision uses a two-full-day release buffer. These economic facts motivate a falsifiable rule, not a claim of alpha.

## Exactly one fee-burden rule and execution

At each **Monday 00:00 UTC** in the account period, let `M` be that UTC date. For each metric day `d`, define the non-price fee burden `B(d)=FeeTotNtv(d)/TxCnt(d)` in ETH per transaction. The most recent permitted day is `M−3` (Friday), whose UTC day ended 48 hours before Monday execution. Define `R=sum(FeeTotNtv, d=M−30..M−3)/sum(TxCnt, same 28 days)` and `P=sum(FeeTotNtv, d=M−58..M−31)/sum(TxCnt, same 28 days)`. Target **100% ETH iff `R>P`**, else 100% USDT; equality is cash. This ratio of sums weights transactions, avoiding a zero-volume daily average. Require all 56 daily values, with no price or future metric used by the signal. Before the first Monday hold cash. Maintain target until it changes; do not add discretionary exits.
