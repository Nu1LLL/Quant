## Economic mechanism and limits

An increasing share of BTC turnover in USD-M perpetual futures relative to spot may represent more short-lived, potentially leveraged activity than direct spot demand. **Frozen prediction:** when the recent 28-day perpetual/spot quote-volume ratio is *lower* than the preceding 28-day ratio, subsequent BTC spot returns are more favorable; otherwise cash reduces downside. This is distinct from prior futures **position growth**, funding carry, basis, price trend and standalone spot order-flow tests: it measures **relative realized turnover across two Binance markets**. It is not statistically orthogonal to market activity or volatility. Futures volume includes hedging and market making, does **not** identify leverage, net long/short positioning, open interest, forced liquidation or unique investors; the predicted sign is uncertain. [Research on perpetuals and spot market quality](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/4218907.pdf?abstractid=4218907&mirid=1&type=2) reports both greater spot volume and wider spreads after perpetual listings, motivating caution rather than proving this trading rule. The [Binance public archive documentation](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data) defines the official source format. No paper result is treated as independent OOS for the frozen rule.

Evidence grade: `preregistered_retrospective_official_timestamped_turnover`; `independent_executable_oos=false`. Historical Binance archive bars have intrinsic UTC timestamps and official hashes, unlike current-vintage retrospectively labeled event catalogs. Nonetheless our new 2024–2025 holdout is a **retrospective temporal holdout**, prior BTC price history is known to the research program, and bar OHLCV cannot prove bid/ask depth, queue position or actual fills. ## Exactly one ratio rule and execution

At every **Monday 00:00 UTC** in 2021–2025, define `Vf(d)` and `Vs(d)` as the **completed UTC day's** USD-M BTCUSDT perpetual and spot BTCUSDT `quote_volume` in USDT. Require both strictly positive and finite for each of the preceding 56 days. Define `R_recent = sum(Vf(d), d=Monday−28..Monday−1) / sum(Vs(d), same dates)` and `R_prior = sum(Vf(d), d=Monday−56..Monday−29) / sum(Vs(d), same dates)`. Target **100% BTC spot iff `R_recent < R_prior`**, else **100% USDT cash**; equality is cash. Before the first Monday hold cash. Keep the target until it changes. The rule uses volume only: no price return, funding rate, open interest, look-ahead rank, ML fit or manual trade override.

Execute a changed target at that Monday's BTC spot daily open. One-day-latency stress uses Tuesday open for the *same* Monday decision. Force-sell remaining BTC at 2026-01-01 00:00 UTC first open, assigning proceeds to the 2025-12-31 factor. Every fill pays **10 bp taker fee** plus **5 bp adverse slippage**: buy at `open×1.0005`, sell at `open×0.9995`. Reserve fee, round BTC quantity down to **0.00001 BTC**, retain USDT dust, require ≥10 USDT per fill. Proposed buy notional must be ≤**1% of the prior 30 completed spot UTC days' median quote volume**.
