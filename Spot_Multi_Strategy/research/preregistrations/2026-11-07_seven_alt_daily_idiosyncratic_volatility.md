## Hypothesis and evidence boundary

[Günther, Fieberg and Poddig (2020), *The Cross-Section of Cryptocurrency Risk and Return*](https://www.econstor.eu/bitstream/10419/270511/1/10_3790_vjh_89_4_07.pdf) find a positive **gross** high-minus-low spread for six-month CAPM-residual standard deviation in a broad 2013–2019 cryptocurrency cross section. Their mechanism is compensation for coin-specific risk that marginal investors cannot fully diversify. It is economically separate from the already rejected *conditional market downside beta*, raw return skewness, weekday demand, Amihud illiquidity, funding carry, pair distance, jump reversal and short-horizon flow mechanisms: residual dispersion is measured **after** removing the common BTC market component. A separate 2020 study found no conventional crypto low-volatility anomaly; the high-minus-low direction here follows the former paper and is frozen before any new strategy result.

This is an explicit **transfer**, not a replication: the paper uses a broad equally weighted market proxy, many coins and daily quintiles; here BTC is the fixed market proxy, seven archive-screened Binance USD-M alt contracts are candidates and the upper/lower two are traded. The paper's cost-free gross spread is neither a target return nor execution evidence. Previous tests on the same 2021–2026 history mean this is not an independent program-level OOS discovery.

## Frozen daily signal, eligibility, execution and account

At each UTC decision boundary `d 00:00`, construct exactly **182 completed, non-overlapping daily log returns** for BTC and each alt from last completed `23:55` five-minute closes. The latest return ends immediately before `d`; oldest boundary precedes `d` by 182 days. The current `d 00:00` bar, entry and held-period bars never enter the signal. For each alt fit `r_i = alpha_i + beta_i*r_BTC + residual_i` by two-column OLS over those 182 paired returns. Define `IVOL_i = sqrt(sum(residual_i^2)/(182-2))`. A zero BTC variance, rank-deficient design, nonfinite input or nonfinite score is a full data/signal failure, not grounds to drop a day or coin. Sort eligible alts by `(IVOL_i, symbol)` ascending; **long the two highest and short the two lowest**, lexical ties fixed.

Eligibility is recomputed before every rank from only the **previous completed UTC day's** 288 five-minute quote-volume bars. For a candidate alt, their median must be at least `50 × (0.25 × current baseline-account equity)`, forecasting no more than 2% participation for a $2,500 starting leg. All seven alts remain in the screened universe; this pre-signal capacity filter does not use entry/exit/future volume. If fewer than four qualify, hold cash that day and record the reason. A selected coin must still meet the same filter; no substitution after ranking. The eligibility design reflects $10,000 account size and prior source-liquidity diagnostics, so it does not restore independence from previous program-level data inspection.

Start with **$10,000**. Trade four equal 25%-of-current-equity legs, gross **1x**, initial net zero, always below the user's 5x hard cap. Enter at `d 00:05` archived open and fully exit at `(d+1) 00:05` archived open; flatten and reopen even if a symbol persists. Baseline cost is **5 bp taker fee plus 5 bp adverse slippage per fill**; charge/credit every actual verified funding event between entry (inclusive) and exit (exclusive), using its contemporaneous archived open. No interest, external deposits, Kelly scaling, optimizer, stop, take-profit or market-state filter. Mark regular equity at every five-minute close and adverse long-low/short-high combined mark; nonpositive equity is failure. Report daily net factor grid, 63 calendar months, gross executed-price PnL, fees, funding, exact account reconciliation and ex-post entry/exit participation. Four fixed scenarios share identical baseline decisions/eligibility: `base_1x`, `double_slippage_1x` (10 bp adverse slippage), `extra_bar_latency_1x` (entry at `00:10`, same exit), and `no_funding_credit_1x` (debits retained, favorable funding credits suppressed).
