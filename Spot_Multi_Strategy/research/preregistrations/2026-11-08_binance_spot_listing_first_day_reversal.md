# Preregistration: Binance spot first-day listing sell-off reversal

**Experiment ID:** `binance_spot_listing_first_day_reversal_2026_11_08`. This file is committed and pushed **by itself before opening a single event-window price bar or calculating any return**. One fixed long/cash rule tests whether the first trading day's selling of a newly listed floating-price token overreacts and partially reverses over the following three days. This is an *inference* from the market-access and attention mechanism in [Ante's exchange-listing event study](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3450301), not a claim that the paper established this exact trade. It differs economically from the already rejected *new perpetual short-access* launch test: this one studies existing spot owners' first-day selling and can trade only spot long. No past listing return, sign, trade, or Gate metric has been read to choose this rule.

## Frozen causal rule and $10,000 ledger

- Start with **$10,000 USDT cash**. Sort events by `(t0, symbol)`, never choose by future price or liquidity. No leverage or borrowing: spot position gross notional is **25% of current marked equity**, 1x asset leverage, far below the user's 5x ceiling. Only **one** position at a time; later eligible events whose entry falls before the current scheduled/actual exit are skipped, lexical symbol order breaks ties.
- Observe the **first 24 completed hourly bars** `[t0,t0+24h)`. First-hour close is known at `t0+1h`, and the 24th close is known at `t0+24h`. Go long only when `close_24th / close_1st <= 0.90`. No entry on any other sign. Use the last six completed hours' **median USDT quote volume** as the pre-entry capacity proxy. Require median hourly quote volume ≥100× proposed spot notional (≤1% of an hour's median volume); otherwise skip. These fixed thresholds are not adjusted after result viewing.
- Buy at the **open of the `t0+24h` bar** with adverse **50 bp slippage** plus **10 bp taker fee**. Sell at the **open of the `t0+96h` bar** with adverse 50 bp slippage and 10 bp fee. No funding, rewards or interest are claimed. If a held hourly bar's low touches **85% of the actual filled entry**, sell at the next bar's open using `min(next_open, trigger_low, 0.85×entry_fill)` then another 50 bp adverse slippage; this pessimistic stop permits losses beyond 15% on gaps. Final held-bar triggers exit at the scheduled exit. Charge fees on actual filled notional both ways. Do not permit fractional capital beyond available cash, net deposits, token borrowing, Kelly resizing, take-profit, switching assets or discretionary exceptions.
- Record hourly closing account equity and hourly adverse intrabar low marks; maintain cash between trades. Produce UTC daily closing equity for all 1,826 days, including zero-return cash days, and conservative maximum drawdown from the running peak to each adverse mark. Daily Sharpe uses mean/std of simple net daily returns times `sqrt(365)`, sample std. CAGR uses elapsed 1,826 days and 365.25-day annualization. Profit Factor is net daily dollar gains divided by absolute net daily dollar losses; undefined is a failure. Reconcile every trade's gross price PnL and both fees exactly to terminal equity.

## Fixed validation, robustness, and stopping

- Full numerical Gate: net CAGR **≥40%** (40–60% target), Sharpe **>1.5**, adverse-mark MDD **≤20%**, daily-dollar PF **>1.3**; at least **30 executed trades**; **≥36/60** strictly positive months and longest nonpositive streak **≤3**; each of five annual folds has ≥1 trade, positive net return and positive Sharpe. Any zero-trade, zero-variance or undefined statistic fails.
- Four BTC states at each entry use only the preceding 31 completed UTC-day BTCUSDT closes: 30-day log-price sum positive/nonpositive × annualized sample log-return volatility ≥80%/<80%. BTC daily spot ZIPs from 2020-12 through 2025-12, each CHECKSUM verified, provide the regime source. Require ≥5 executed trades and positive cumulative net dollar PnL in **each** of four states. Missing BTC dates/source is a failure.
- Exactly **5,000** NumPy PCG64 circular **21-day block bootstrap** paths on the full net daily return/peak/trough series, seed **20261109**. Require **≥80%** paths jointly meeting the four numerical CAGR/Sharpe/MDD/PF thresholds; record ruined and undefined paths plus p05/p50/p95. Also rerun the same selected events with **100 bp** slippage per side and with entry delayed one hour (six-hour capacity decision remains at original time); each stress must end above $10,000. Neither stress may reselect events.
- If any data, numerical, annual, monthly, regime, Monte Carlo, stress or independent-OOS Gate fails, seal this rule as **rejected** on 2021–2025. Do **not** reverse its direction, alter the 10% signal, 72h hold, 15% stop, universe, dates, costs, cash allocation, thresholds, or leverage on the same historical window. A positive component result is only a research input for a separately preregistered future combination, never this experiment's success. No live order is placed.
