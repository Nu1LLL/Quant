## Independent economic hypothesis and transfer limits

[Dobrynskaya (September 2020), *Is Downside Risk Priced In Cryptocurrency Market?*](https://wp.hse.ru/data/2020/09/16/1583081932/79FE2020.pdf) reports that coins with higher beta to **negative** cryptocurrency-market weeks earned more than low-downside-beta coins in a 2014–2018 cross section. The mechanism is compensation for loss when aggregate wealth is falling. It is distinct from the prior rejected lottery/skewness demand, same-weekday seasonal demand, single-coin jumps, lead-lag, funding carry, pair distance, order-flow reversal and liquidity premium hypotheses. This is a **new transfer test**, not a replication: the paper uses a broad capitalization-weighted coin-market index, up to hundreds of coins and largely non-executable historical prices. Here the fixed BTC USD-M weekly return is the market proxy and eight source-quality-screened Binance USD-M contracts are executable candidates. The paper's gross 2.6% weekly high-minus-low figure is not a forecast for this account or evidence after fees/funding. No upside-beta, regular-beta or BTC-short substitution is tested.

## Frozen signal and weekly execution

At each formal Friday `d 00:00 UTC`, use exactly **26 completed non-overlapping seven-day log-return pairs**, all known at the last archived five-minute close of Thursday `d-1` at `23:55`. Pair `k` ends at `d-7k` and starts at `d-7(k+1)`, for `k=0..25`; a boundary price means the **last completed five-minute close immediately before that UTC date**, so the oldest required close precedes 2021-01-08 for the first trade. Define market log return `m_k` from `BTCUSDT` and coin log return `r_{i,k}` the same way. No current entry/held-period bar enters the signal. Use zero as the downside boundary, as in the cited paper. If fewer than **five** of 26 market returns are `<0` or fewer than five are `>0`, hold all cash for the week and record the reason. If market returns are zero for any remaining week, include them in the regression but neither count them as positive nor negative.

For each coin fit the fixed three-column OLS regression over these 26 pairs: `r_i = alpha_i + beta_minus_i * m + gamma_i * (m * 1[m>0]) + error_i`. The slope `beta_minus_i` is its downside beta. Reject any nonfinite input or rank-deficient design as a **full experiment failure**, not a candidate for ad hoc parameters. Sort `(beta_minus, symbol)` ascending; **short two lowest, long two highest** with lexical ties. This implements the cited paper's sign and conditional-beta form; no regular-beta subtractor, signal threshold, trend filter, ML selection, Kelly sizing, stop or take-profit.

Each selected leg has 25% of current baseline/scenario equity, giving **1.0x gross, zero initial net**, under the user's hard 5x cap. At `d 00:00`, median of each selected coin's 288 five-minute **quote-volume bars on the preceding completed UTC day** must be at least `50 * baseline-account leg notional`, forecasting ≤2% participation. If one fails, all four legs stay cash for seven days; the identical baseline capacity/selection schedule is replayed under every stress. Never use future entry/exit bar volume for admission; report it only as an ex-post fill diagnostic.

Enter four legs at Friday `00:05` archived open and fully exit seven days later at next Friday `00:05` archived open. Fully close and reopen even if a symbol persists. Charge **5 bp taker fee plus 5 bp adverse slippage per fill**. Charge/credit every verified actual funding event from entry through before exit at its contemporaneous archived open. Start with **$10,000** and no external deposits, cash yield or off-account compounding. Mark at every five-minute regular close and simultaneous adverse long-low/short-high; nonpositive equity fails. Daily account records use the already frozen `[d 00:05, (d+1) 00:05]` convention: exit cost at the right endpoint belongs to the preceding labeled daily interval and a new entry at that instant belongs to the next. Report 63 calendar months, all cash dates, price PnL, fees, funding and exact equity reconciliation.

Four frozen account scenarios use the same signals and capacity schedule: `base_1x` (5-bp slippage), `double_slippage_1x` (10-bp slippage), `extra_bar_latency_1x` (entry at Friday `00:10`, same exit), and `no_funding_credit_1x` (debits charged, favorable credits suppressed). Each stress must finish above $10,000. No scenario or leverage is selected post hoc.

## Gate and stopping rules

The full-window **net** numerical thresholds are CAGR ≥40% (40–60% preferred), daily Sharpe >1.5, conservative five-minute max drawdown ≤20%, and daily dollar-PnL Profit Factor >1.3. At least **150 of 272** scheduled weeks must trade. Every one of five complete annual folds must have a traded day, positive net return and positive daily Sharpe; the terminal 84 days must be profitable. Require at least **38 of 63 positive months** and longest losing-month streak ≤3.

Use the fixed pre-entry BTC 30-day return sign × trailing 30-day sample-volatility relative to prior 90 rolling-volatility median for four bull/bear × high/low states; ties bear/low. Attribute each daily account PnL to its labeled day's state, including held-week days. Each state needs ≥90 formal days and positive aggregate net dollar PnL. Run exactly **5,000 NumPy PCG64 circular 21-day block-bootstrap paths**, seed **20261115**, on all 1,910 daily factors and compressed adverse marks; ≥80% must jointly pass the same four numerical thresholds, and report p05/p50/p95 plus undefined/ruined counts.

Any source, score/regression, account-ruin, fold/state/month, or PnL-reconciliation failure and any numerical/stress Gate failure seals this one-shot test. If all numerical tests pass, the absent prospective executable OOS still keeps the complete Gate closed; at most consider a new small-size paper period, never automatic live orders.
