## Mechanism and evidence grade

It does **not** show that a weekly change in Binance futures average trade size itself predicts returns. This deliberately lower-prior transfer tests whether **abnormally large average trade size in a coin forecasts lower next-week relative return** as hedging pressure unwinds. It is distinct from prior taker-buy/sell *directional* flow reversal, volume-only illiquidity, price momentum/reversal, funding carry, GitHub software issues and macro index levels.
