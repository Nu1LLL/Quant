# Pre-result protocol interpretation (2026-11-02)

This document is committed **before downloading or reading any full-period price/funding archive or strategy return**. It resolves accounting and the stricter already-existing Mini-Medallion validation protocol v2; it does not change the four instruments, flow formula, dates, rank, 04:00 execution, 1× exposure, fee, or slippage frozen in `PREREGISTRATION.md`. No pre-existing strategy or report is edited.

1. **Final boundary.** No new target position is entered at 2026-09-30 04:00 UTC. Positions opened at 2026-09-29 04:00 are fully liquidated at the 2026-09-30 04:00 trade open, paying the same fee and adverse slippage; the remainder of 2026-09-30 is cash, including in September and full-window metrics. Jan 1, 2021 is cash because Dec 31, 2020 is outside the frozen signal window. This avoids requiring an October 2026 archive or omitting final exit costs.
2. **Settlement/accounting clock.** A funding event exactly at 04:00 applies to the *pre-rebalance* position; otherwise it applies to the position held at its actual event time. Daily and monthly formal returns use UTC 00:00-to-00:00 account equity, with a final valuation at 2026-10-01 00:00. All actual fees, slippage, and funding are reflected in equity when they occur. The preregistered "trade-level" PF is computed over nonempty 04:00-to-next-04:00 **portfolio decision cycles** using that cycle's net equity-dollar P&L, including its entry/rebalance costs and assigning the Sep 30 final exit cost to the cycle ending then; this avoids arbitrary per-coin FIFO or partial-lot allocation. The controlling formal Gate PF, per the pre-existing v2 protocol, is instead the sum of positive **UTC calendar-day net returns** divided by the magnitude of negative calendar-day net returns. Both must be reported.
3. **Intrabar drawdown.** Four-hour mark OHLC cannot establish whether different coins' extrema coincided. For each 4h interval, evaluate a deliberately conservative simultaneous adverse scenario: mark **low** for every long and mark **high** for every short, against the running peak from observed mark closes and post-cost cash flows. Label the result an OHLC worst-case bound, not a reconstructed tick-level drawdown. The final Gate uses this pessimistic value; no favorable same-bar extrema are used to raise the peak.
4. **Regimes.** Bull/bear/sideways must each be reported with lagged 30-day BTC spot labels, observation/exposure counts, net return, daily Sharpe and daily PF. The existing v2 protocol requires complete causal regime reporting, not positive returns in every regime. The earlier words "regime checks ... pass" mean the labeling/reporting completeness check only; no post-result per-regime threshold is created.
5. **Walk-forward and Monte Carlo precedence.** The preregistered 2022–2025 annual panel and 30-day/10,000-block bootstrap are retained as *secondary* diagnostics. But 2022–2023 are development-description years, **not** OOS folds. The 2024–Sep 2026 holdout contains only two complete calendar OOS years (2024 and 2025), and its duration is about 2.75 years. The protected protocol v2 requires at least five years of truly independent OOS and five complete OOS yearly folds, with at least 80% of folds jointly net-profitable, daily PF>1, and fold MDD≥−20%. Thus this frozen experiment **cannot pass the full unified Gate regardless of its numeric outcome**. Do not relabel 2021–2023 as independent, lengthen the window, or change dates after results. Also run and report the v2-required 21-day circular block bootstrap (at least 5,000 paths; use the same 10,000 and seed 20261102) with 5th percentiles and four-metric joint-pass probability including daily PF>1.3. Both bootstraps use net returns, zero-return days, fees, slippage, and funding.
6. **Leverage and evidence.** This run's frozen primary result is 1× only. The preregistration's conditional 3×/5× scenarios cannot be run or interpreted as executable until maintenance margin, liquidation fee, and funding/leverage mechanics are separately frozen before seeing those scenario outcomes. They cannot rescue failed 1× Sharpe or the insufficient OOS span. This study remains a **retrospective, bar-level exploratory candidate**; it authorizes no real orders.

The paper's global fiat-currency order flows and nonlinear ML are not the same data or model as Binance's single-venue spot taker-buy volume. It supplies a mechanism hypothesis only. The earlier L1 OFI feature code belongs to the broad order-flow family but did not produce a formal test of this exact cross-market daily rule. This evidence distinction must appear in the final report.
