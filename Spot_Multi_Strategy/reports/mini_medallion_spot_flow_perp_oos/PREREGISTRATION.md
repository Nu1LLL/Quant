# Spot aggressive-flow → perpetual-futures return: frozen protocol

Registered on 2026-11-02 **before reading this rule's returns or any full-period price/funding rows**. This is one new economic hypothesis, not an extension or retuning of the prior volume-reversal, basis, funding, or trend studies. The existing OFI-v2 work validated L1 order-book feature plumbing but did not test taker-volume alpha. The 2026 order-flow paper motivates the mechanism; it does **not** establish that this exact Binance strategy is profitable.

## Fixed inputs and causal rule

- Instruments: Binance BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, XRPUSDT, LTCUSDT **spot** for the signal; the same four Binance USDⓈ-M perpetual contracts for execution, marking, and actual funding. These four liquid, pre-2021 contracts are fixed now. No replacement, dropping, or post-result addition is allowed.
- Source: official monthly Binance Data Vision ZIPs and their official SHA-256 `.CHECKSUM` files: spot 4h `klines`; USDⓈ-M 4h `klines`, 4h `markPriceKlines`, and `fundingRate`. Do not commit raw bars. The first and last month URLs were checked for existence only; this is not a completeness or return check. Spot timestamps switch from milliseconds to microseconds in 2025, which the parser must distinguish explicitly.
- Frozen window: 2021-01-01 00:00 UTC through 2026-10-01 00:00 UTC, i.e. 69 complete months. Development-description window 2021-01-01 through 2023-12-31; **untouched historical holdout** 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-30. Both use the same fixed rule: there is no fitted parameter, asset selection, or threshold optimization. The first trade may occur only after a complete prior UTC day is observable.
- At the end of UTC day D, aggregate its six complete spot 4h bars for each coin: `Q=sum(quote_asset_volume)`, `B=sum(taker_buy_quote_asset_volume)`, `flow=(2*B-Q)/Q`. Zero/invalid Q is a data failure, not a neutral signal. Only day D's completed bars may inform day D+1 orders.
- At **04:00 UTC on D+1**, rank the four `flow` values descending; ties break by symbol alphabetically. Buy one perpetual with highest flow and sell one with lowest flow; the middle two are cash. Each active leg has 50% of current account equity notional, so gross exposure is 1× and signed net exposure is zero at entry. At the next 04:00 UTC, rebalance to the newly determined pair and 50% weights. Unchanged contracts are adjusted only for weight drift; transaction costs apply to **actual absolute notional turnover**, not automatically to four fictitious round trips. No extra stop, filter, or signal flip.
- Execution uses each perpetual's actual 04:00 4h trade **open**; the full preceding spot day ended at 00:00, leaving a four-hour information/latency buffer. One-sided taker fee is fixed at 5 basis points and adverse execution slippage at 10 basis points per traded notional; stress case uses 25 basis points slippage. Funding cash flows use each actual historical funding event while the position is open; no averaged or assumed fixed funding. Uninvested collateral earns zero. Initial capital is USD 10,000. No borrowing or stock data is used in this experiment.
- Mark-to-market and drawdown use perpetual mark prices, with 4h mark OHLC used for adverse intrabar drawdown checks; realized entry/exit uses trade open plus slippage. Cross-month price/position continuity is mandatory. Financing other than actual funding is zero at 1×. If a market-order fill, exchange outage, or liquidity impact cannot be independently reconstructed from 4h candles, the outcome is labeled a **bar-level simulation**, not executable proof.

## Data and stopping gates

1. Before computing any strategy P&L, verify the official checksum of **every** required monthly archive, exact expected CSV schema, positive/ordered OHLC, nonnegative volumes, `0 ≤ B ≤ Q`, UTC 4h alignment, no duplicate times, and monotone timestamps. Every spot/trade/mark series for every fixed symbol must cover **100%** of its expected 4h grid: one missing bar is a data failure, not a day to skip or forward-fill. Funding must have no duplicate events, no gap over 24 hours, and no missing first/last 24 hours. A failed download after bounded retries or failed data gate **seals the whole hypothesis as data-ineligible**; do not substitute an asset, year, vendor, or less strict threshold.
2. If data pass, run exactly this rule once. Report net full-history and holdout CAGR, daily-return Sharpe (365-day convention), worst intrabar mark-to-market drawdown, trade-level profit factor, turnover, funding, fees, slippage, and monthly net returns. Also report the share of positive holdout months and the worst holdout month; stable monthly gains require at least 70% positive holdout months and no month worse than −10%. Do not silently exclude partial 2026 (nine months), market crashes, or flat months.
3. Annual chronological walk-forward: separately report every complete calendar year 2022–2025 and Jan–Sep 2026 using only prior information; at least 80% of the four complete years must have positive net return and PF>1, and Jan–Sep 2026 must be positive. Regimes are fixed from *prior* 30-day BTC spot returns: bull >+10%, bear <−10%, otherwise sideways; report each regime's net return, Sharpe, and exposure without changing the rule.
4. Block Monte Carlo: 10,000 deterministic-seed (20261102) resamples of holdout **daily** net strategy returns using contiguous 30-day blocks, retaining zero-return days and cost/funding. Report the fraction jointly satisfying CAGR≥40%, Sharpe>1.5, and drawdown no worse than −20%; the joint lower-confidence criterion is at least 5% of resamples, and separately 95% of resamples must have positive CAGR. These are stability diagnostics, not an independent future test.
5. Historical numeric Gate requires **both** full-history and holdout net CAGR≥40%, Sharpe>1.5, drawdown≥−20%, PF>1.3; the monthly, annual, regime, and Monte Carlo checks above must also pass. The desired 40–60% CAGR band is reported, but >60% is not treated as a failure by itself; it is flagged for leverage/concentration review. Any failed item rejects the candidate. 3× and 5× are **not** tested unless the 1× full and holdout Sharpe and PF already pass; even then they are only stress cases under conservative liquidation and 25bp-per-side slippage, never a way to rescue weak 1× Sharpe. Exposure must never exceed the user's 5× ceiling.
6. Evidence grade: this is a frozen **retrospective historical holdout**, not genuinely prospective independent OOS. Even if all historical numbers pass, the full Gate remains unverified until fresh post-registration paper data and small-size simulated execution validate the same frozen rule. No automatic real trading, order submission, or claim of stable income follows from this backtest.

Failure ends this particular hypothesis. Any later idea must have a distinct economic rationale and a separately committed preregistration; do not tune this rule on the revealed years, costs, assets, delay, or holding period.

Sources: [Binance public archive schema, timestamp units and checksums](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data); [Anastasopoulos et al., *Order flow and cryptocurrency returns*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.finmar.2026.101047). The cited paper motivates flow information only; it is not this study's outcome.
